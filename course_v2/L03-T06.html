Turning Left Across Traffic: Complete Yield Mandate

An operator intending to turn left within an intersection or into an alley, private road, or driveway SHALL YIELD the right-of-way to a vehicle that is approaching from the opposite direction and is within the intersection or so close as to present an immediate hazard.

Bodily Injury Collision $500 to $2,000 Fine TTC § 542.4045(a) + 2 Points
Serious Bodily Injury $1,000 to $4,000 Fine TTC § 542.4045(b) + 180d Susp.
Texas Defensive Principle: Right-of-way is given, not taken. Even with a green phase, you must confirm opposing gaps before committing turning momentum.

CRIMINAL SANCTION ZONE Texas Transp. Code § 545.066

Passing a Stopped School Bus with Flashing Red Signals

Drivers approaching from EITHER DIRECTION on an undivided roadway must halt before reaching the school bus when alternating flashing red lights are displayed, and may not proceed until the bus resumes motion, signals are deactivated, or the driver is visually signaled to proceed.

1st Offense Fine $500 to $1,250 Misdemeanor Citation
Repeat Offense / Injury State Jail Felony Up to 2 yrs jail + $10,000 fine
Median Barrier Rule: Oncoming traffic is ONLY exempt if separated by an engineered physical barrier or unpaved median. A center turn lane is NOT a physical barrier.

Texas Right-of-Way Statutes & Penalties

  • 01. The Right-of-Way is Given, Not Taken: Even if you legally have right-of-way, you must yield if another driver fails to do so to prevent a collision. Safety always supersedes legal priority.
  • 02. Bodily Injury Surcharge (TTC § 542.4045): Failing to yield right-of-way resulting in bodily injury carries a mandatory $500–$2,000 fine; if serious bodily injury occurs, the fine escalates to $1,000–$4,000.
  • 03. School Buses (TTC § 545.066): Failing to stop for alternately flashing red lights carries a $500–$1,250 fine for a first offense, escalating to a State Jail Felony for repeated injury crashes.

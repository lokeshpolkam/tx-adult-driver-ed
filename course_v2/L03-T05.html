The Three-Point Turn (K-Turn) & 500-Ft U-Turn Caliper

The Three-Point Turn (K-Turn) is an essential precision maneuver executed to reverse direction on narrow two-lane roadways where driveways and U-turn opportunities are absent. Drivers must maintain a strict 4-stage protocol, utilizing complete steering lock sweeps and continuous 360-degree traffic scans.

4-STAGE K-TURN PROTOCOL

1. Pull to right curb & stop.
2. Full lock LEFT (+35°) forward.
3. Full lock RIGHT (-35°) reverse.
4. Straighten wheel & drive out.

500-FT U-TURN RULE

Under TTC § 545.162, U-turns are illegal on curves or hill crests where approaching drivers cannot see you from 500 feet in either direction.

Parallel Parking 18-Inch Rule & Statutory Buffer Zones

Parallel parking is a mandatory evaluation on the Texas DPS Driving Skills Exam. Texas Transportation Code § 545.303(a) explicitly mandates that on a two-way roadway, your vehicle's right-hand wheels must be parallel to and within 18 inches (1.5 feet) of the right-hand curb or edge of roadway. Recommended optimal distance is 6 inches.

Turn Front Wheels LEFT (Away from Raised Curb)

When facing uphill with a raised curb, turn your front wheels sharply to the LEFT (away from the curb). Let the vehicle roll back gently until the back of the front right tire rests against the curb face. If brakes fail, the curb physically wedges the wheel.

DRIVER RESPONSIBILITY TTC § 545.404

Unattended Motor Vehicle Legal Protocol

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.404, an operator may not permit a motor vehicle to stand unattended without executing 5 mandatory safety steps:

  • 1 Stop the engine completely.
  • 2 Lock the ignition to prevent unauthorized movement.
  • 3 Remove the key from the ignition cylinder.
  • 4 Set the parking brake effectively.
  • 5 If standing on a grade, turn the front wheels to the curb or side of the roadway.

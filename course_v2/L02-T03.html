Driver License Sanctions & Disqualifications

Suspensions, Revocations, Cancellations, ALR Implied Consent & Criminal DWLI Escalation

Operating a motor vehicle on Texas public roadways is a conditional administrative privilege under Texas Transportation Code § 521.021, not a constitutional property right. The State of Texas exercises strict authority through the Department of Public Safety (DPS) and judicial tribunals to pause, terminate, void, or deny driving privileges when statutory covenants are breached.

gavel Dual Administrative & Judicial Jurisdiction:

License sanctions operate on two parallel, independent tracks: Administrative Disqualifications enforced directly by the DPS mainframe (points, ALR chemical refusals, Medical Advisory Board holds) and Judicial Mandates triggered automatically by court conviction abstracts (DWI, manslaughter, felony evading). An acquittal in criminal court does not vacate civil administrative suspensions.

STATUTORY CAUSES // DISQUALIFICATION MATRIX TTC §§ 521.292 – 521.344

Judicial Mandates vs. DPS Administrative Audits

MANDATORY JUDICIAL (TTC § 521.341) AUTOMATIC SUSPENSION

Upon receipt of a certified abstract of court conviction, DPS is statutorily mandated to suspend the driver license without discretionary hearing for:

  • Driving While Intoxicated (DWI) or Boating While Intoxicated
  • Controlled Substances Act drug offenses (HSC Ch. 481)
  • Intoxication Assault (§ 49.07) or Intoxication Manslaughter (§ 49.08)
  • Failure to Stop and Render Aid (Hit-and-Run involving injury/death)
  • Subsequent conviction for passing a stopped school bus
  • Evading arrest in a motor vehicle or racing on a public highway
  • Fictitious license plates, inspection certificates, or fraudulent government records
DPS ADMINISTRATIVE (TTC § 521.292) 20-DAY HEARING WINDOW

DPS possesses independent authority to suspend or revoke driving privileges after notice and opportunity for a formal administrative hearing for:

  • Habitual Traffic Violator: Conviction of 4 or more moving violations in 12 consecutive months, or 7 or more in 24 months
  • Causing a collision resulting in serious bodily injury or death
  • Failing or refusing to undergo an evaluation required by the Medical Advisory Board (MAB)
  • Committing an offense out-of-state that would trigger suspension in Texas
PROCEDURE: DPS mails Notice of Suspension. Licensee has exactly 20 calendar days to file a written hearing request. Failure waives hearing rights.
IMPLIED CONSENT // CIVIL ENFORCEMENT TTC CH. 524 & 724

Administrative License Revocation (ALR)

Under Texas Implied Consent law, anyone arrested for operating a motor vehicle or watercraft in a public place while intoxicated is deemed to have consented to submit breath or blood specimens for chemical analysis.

JUDICIAL RELIEF // RESTRICTED ACCESS TTC §§ 521.241 – 521.253

Occupational Driver License (ODL) Mechanics

An Occupational License (Essential Need License) is a special restricted license granted by a county, district, or justice court allowing a suspended driver to operate exclusively for essential work, educational attendance, or vital household duties.

warning Rigid Operational Restrictions & Class B Violation:

Under TTC § 521.253, an ODL authorizes driving strictly within specified hours (maximum 4 hours per day standard, up to 12 hours on proof of necessity) and approved routes. Operating outside authorized hours or routes is a Class B misdemeanor and results in immediate, permanent judicial revocation of the ODL order! An ODL can never authorize operation of a Commercial Motor Vehicle (CMV).

3RD DWI OFFENSE (3RD DEGREE FELONY) FINE ≤ $10,000
Confinement: 2 to 10 years in TDCJ State Prison. Suspension: 180 days to 2 years. Felony criminal record.

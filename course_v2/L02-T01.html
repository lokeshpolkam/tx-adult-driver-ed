Texas Driver License Intake, Eligibility & Renewals

Process of Obtaining, Possessing, and Renewing a Texas Driver License

Operating a motor vehicle on Texas public roadways is a regulated legal privilege granted by the State under Texas Transportation Code Chapter 521. Maintaining uninterrupted driving authorization requires strict compliance with statutory intake protocols, REAL ID identity verification, examination benchmarks, and mandatory renewal intervals.

verified_user General Licensing Mandate (TTC § 521.021):

A person may not operate a motor vehicle on a highway in this state unless the person holds a valid driver's license. Operating without a valid license is a Class C misdemeanor. New Texas residents have strictly 90 consecutive days to surrender an out-of-state credential and obtain a Texas license (TTC § 521.029).

TTC § 521.161

Statutory Examination Benchmarks

To receive a Texas Class C license, applicants must demonstrate physical fitness, regulatory mastery, and road competency:

visibility 1. Optical Acuity & Peripheral Scan 20/40 ACUITY

Must achieve at least 20/40 visual acuity with or without corrective lenses, plus 140° total horizontal visual field. Falling below 20/40 triggers Restriction A (corrective lenses required when operating) or ophthalmologist referral.

quiz 2. Knowledge Exam Waiver ≥ 70% PASS RATE

Covers 30 questions on Texas traffic signs, right-of-way, and speed laws. Adults aged 18–24 who successfully pass this ADE-1317 course receive form ADE-964, exempting them from the DPS written knowledge exam.

play_circle 3. Impact Texas Drivers (ITD) 1-HR VIDEO MANDATE

Under TTC § 521.1601, completion of the free 1-hour Impact Texas Adult Drivers (ITAD) distracted driving module is an absolute legal prerequisite before scheduling the road skills exam. The certificate is valid for 90 days.

directions_car 4. DPS Road Skills Test 4 CAPABILITIES

Evaluates Control, Observation, Positioning, and Signaling. Automatic failures occur for hazardous actions, causing a collision, disobeying traffic-control devices, or refusing to follow examiner directions.

In-Person Biometric Capture & Testing

edit_notifications Ongoing Legal Duties
TTC §§ 521.054 & 521.028

Address Changes, Carrying Mandate & Military Service

home_pin 30-Day Address/Name Notice § 521.054

If you change your residential address or legally change your name, you must officially notify the Texas DPS within 30 calendar days. You may update your address online at Texas.gov. Failing to notify DPS within 30 days is a Class C misdemeanor fine of up to $200.

badge Duty to Carry & Exhibit § 521.025

A licensee must have the license in their immediate possession at all times when operating a motor vehicle and display it upon lawful demand of a peace officer. A citation may be dismissed if proof of a valid license at the time of the offense is presented to the court.

military_tech Active Duty Military Extension § 521.028

A Texas driver license held by a person on active duty in the U.S. Armed Forces serving outside Texas remains valid indefinitely throughout continuous military deployment, plus 90 days following honorable discharge.

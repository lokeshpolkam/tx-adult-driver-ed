Texas Highway Yielding Statutes

  • 01. Texas Frontage Road Rule (TTC § 545.154): An operator on an access or frontage road of a limited-access or controlled-access highway SHALL YIELD the right-of-way to a vehicle entering or leaving the highway or about to enter or leave the highway.
  • 02. Simultaneous Multi-Lane Merge (TTC § 545.061): When two vehicles on a road with three or more lanes traveling in the same direction attempt to enter the same lane simultaneously, the driver entering from the right MUST yield to the driver entering from the left.
  • 03. T-Intersections & Driveways (TTC § 545.155): Operators emerging from an alley, private driveway, building, or unpaved roadway must stop and yield right-of-way to all approaching vehicles and pedestrians on the continuous through road.

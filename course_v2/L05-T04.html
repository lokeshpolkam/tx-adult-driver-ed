SCENARIO 1: UPHILL WITH CURB // AWAY FROM CURB TTC § 545.304(a)

Turn Wheels Sharply Left (Away from Curb)

When parked facing uphill on a street with a curb, turn your front wheels sharply to the left (away from the curb). Allow the vehicle to roll back slowly until the back of the front tire touches the concrete curb. If your parking brake fails, gravity pulls the car backward, jamming the tire tread firmly against the curb to stop runaway motion.

The 7 Steps to a Safe Turn, 60-Ft Backing & Coasting Ban

Texas 7-Step Precision Turn Sequence:

  1. Decide early: Never execute an impulsive last-minute turn.
  2. Scan perimeter: Check rearview, side mirrors, and blind spots before lane changes.
  3. Position vehicle: Enter proper turn lane at least one-half block in advance.
  4. Signal 100 feet in advance: Continuously actuate turn lamps (TTC § 545.104).
  5. Brake prior to turn: Decelerate smoothly before entering the arc; never brake or clutch mid-turn.
  6. Maintain dedicated lane: Stay centered without clipping gutters or crossing double yellow lines.
  7. Finish in proper lane: Accelerate smoothly out of the apex into the authorized travel lane.
replay DPS 60-FOOT BACKING TEST

Texas DPS requires backing smoothly in a straight line for 60 feet at low speed. Automated parking aids or auto-pilot features are strictly prohibited during the examination.

do_not_disturb_on COASTING BAN (TTC § 545.406)

It is illegal to coast on a downgrade with gears or transmission in neutral, or with the clutch disengaged on manual transmissions. Low gear engine braking must remain engaged.

01 // ORDER & PREDICTABILITY TTC § 545.051

Right-Half Lane Discipline & Turning Hierarchies

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.051, an operator on a roadway of sufficient width shall drive on the right half of the roadway, except when passing another vehicle, avoiding an obstruction, or traveling on a one-way designated roadway. Universal adherence creates deterministic directional flow vectors, preventing head-on collisions.

STATUTORY PENALTY: Moving violation, Class C Misdemeanor ($1 to $200 fine), and 2 points on DPS driver record.

03 // USER PROTECTION TTC § 545.157 • § 552.003

Move Over / Slow Down Act & Pedestrian Crosswalk Shield

Texas law establishes protective safety corridors for vulnerable humans outside passenger vehicles:

§ 545.157 (Move Over / Slow Down): When passing a stationary authorized emergency vehicle, TxDOT service truck, tow truck, or utility vehicle with flashing lights, drivers must vacate the adjacent lane. If unable to merge safely, drivers MUST reduce vehicle speed by at least 20 mph below the posted speed limit (or 5 mph if speed limit is under 25 mph). Violation fine: up to $200 (up to $2,000 if injury occurs).
§ 552.003 (Pedestrian Crosswalk Priority): The operator of a vehicle shall yield the right-of-way, stopping if necessary, to permit a pedestrian to cross within an unsignalized crosswalk when the pedestrian is on the half of the roadway upon which the vehicle is traveling.
04 // LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY TTC § 601.051 • § 724.011

Financial Responsibility (30/60/25) & Texas Implied Consent

Operating a motor vehicle binds the driver to severe legal and financial obligations under Texas statute:

§ 601.072 (30/60/25 Financial Responsibility): Mandatory minimum liability limits: $30,000 bodily injury per person, $60,000 bodily injury per crash, and $25,000 property damage. Operating without insurance: $175–$350 first offense fine, $1,000 repeat fine, and 180-day vehicle impoundment.
§ 724.011 / § 724.035 (Implied Consent Law): By operating a motor vehicle on Texas public roads, a motorist is deemed to have consented to the taking of one or more chemical specimens (breath or blood) if arrested for DWI. Refusal results in an automatic 180-day administrative license suspension (ALR) for a first refusal (and 2 years for subsequent refusals).

COVENANT // 01 TTC § 545.051
Right-Side Roadway Travel

Operators must travel on the right half of the roadway at all times. Crossing into opposing lanes is lawful only when overtaking, avoiding an obstacle, or following official traffic control signs.

PENALTY: Moving violation + Class C fine ($1 to $200).
COVENANT // 02 TTC § 545.157
Move Over / Slow Down Mandate

Vacate the lane adjacent to stopped emergency, TxDOT, or utility vehicles displaying lights. If unable to vacate, slow down 20 mph below posted limit (or 5 mph if limit < 25 mph).

PENALTY: Fine up to $200; up to $2,000 if injury occurs.
COVENANT // 03 TTC § 724.011
Implied Consent to Testing

By operating on Texas roads, drivers impliedly consent to provide breath or blood specimens if lawfully arrested for DWI. Officers may obtain search warrants for blood upon refusal.

PENALTY: Automatic 180-day license suspension for 1st refusal.
COVENANT // 04 TTC § 601.072
30/60/25 Liability Insurance

Must maintain valid insurance policy: $30k bodily injury per person, $60k per crash, and $25k property damage. Proof must be produced electronically or in print upon request.

PENALTY: $175–$350 fine 1st; $1,000 fine & impound repeat.
COVENANT // 05 TTC § 552.003
Pedestrian Crosswalk Priority

Drivers must stop and yield to pedestrians crossing within any marked or unmarked crosswalk on their half of the road or approaching so closely as to be in danger.

PENALTY: Fine up to $500; criminal charge if collision occurs.
COVENANT // 06 TTC § 545.351
Basic Speed Rule & Prudence

Prohibits operating at a speed greater than reasonable and prudent under environmental conditions. Driving posted 70 mph in torrential rain or heavy fog constitutes illegal speeding.

PENALTY: Moving violation citation scaled by road hazards.

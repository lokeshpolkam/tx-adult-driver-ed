Statutory Medical Signatory Bifurcation (TTC § 502.061)

The Texas Transportation Code establishes an intentional divide between physical conditions and mental/neurological conditions for vehicle registration indicators:

Physical Conditions (§ 502.061(a)):

Limited strictly to licensed physicians (M.D. or D.O.). Deafness, severe hearing impairment, and physical vocal tract mutism require anatomical medical certification.

Mental Health & Neurological Conditions (§ 502.061(b)):

Expands authority to licensed psychologists (under Texas Occupations Code Ch. 501) and licensed non-physician mental health professionals (LPC, LCSW, LMFT), recognizing that specialized mental health practitioners regularly diagnose and manage autism, PTSD, and cognitive trauma.

Tendering Form VTR-216 in Practice

Vehicle owners submit Form VTR-216 directly to their County Tax Assessor-Collector’s office. Key submission rules:

  • Annual Registration: Submit alongside your annual vehicle inspection report and registration fee (no additional charge for the impediment marker).
  • Mid-Year Filing: If mid-cycle, submit by mail or in person to TxDMV Vehicle Titles and Registration Division to have TLETS updated immediately without reissuing registration stickers.
  • Zero Inspection Impact: Vehicle safety inspections (TTC Chapter 548) do not evaluate, record, or inspect communication impediment disclosures.

Real-World Statutory Routing Scenarios

Evaluate how Texas statutes direct applicants to the proper forms and medical certifying authorities:

CASE 01: VETERAN WITH PTSD // VEHICLE REGISTRATION TTC § 502.061(b)

Marcus is a military veteran diagnosed with PTSD. He wishes to have his truck flagged in TLETS to ensure peace officers do not escalate during roadside stops.

Routing Resolution: Form VTR-216 submitted to County Tax Assessor. Eligible certifiers include Marcus’s VA licensed clinical social worker (LCSW), licensed psychologist, or physician.
CASE 02: MOTORIST WITH DEAFNESS // CAR TITLE TTC § 502.061(a)

Elena has profound physiological deafness. She submits Form VTR-216 so patrol officers are alerted prior to approaching her car window.

Routing Resolution: Because deafness is a physical health condition under § 502.061(a), her form MUST be certified by an M.D. or D.O. Non-physician mental health professionals are statutorily barred.
CASE 03: TEEN DRIVER // NEW CREDENTIAL TTC § 521.142(h)

Tyler is 18 and obtaining his first Texas Class C driver license. He wants protection regardless of whose car he is driving.

Routing Resolution: Tyler must submit Form DL-101 (signed by a physician) in person at Texas DPS. This places Restriction Code 08 on his physical license, which follows him everywhere.

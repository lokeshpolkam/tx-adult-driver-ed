Communication Impediment Program & Occupational Licensing

hearing Communication Impediment Registry DL-101 / VTR-216

Texas provides an innovative program to safeguard individuals with communication challenges (such as autism spectrum disorder, PTSD, expressive language disorders, stuttering, or severe hearing impairment) during interactions with law enforcement:

  • Form DL-101: Physician certification places a restriction code notation directly on the face of the driver license.
  • Form VTR-216: Registers the communication impediment with TxDMV, linking it to the vehicle's license plate. When an officer runs the plate in the patrol car via TLETS prior to approaching, the terminal alerts the officer to the condition, preventing tragic misunderstandings of non-responsiveness or slow compliance.
work Occupational Driver License (ODL) TTC § 521.241–253

An Occupational Driver License (also called an essential need license) is a special restricted license granted by a county or district court judge to an individual whose license is suspended. Key statutory rules include:

  • Essential Need Only: Authorizes driving strictly for employment, educational classes, or vital household/medical duties (max 4 to 12 hours/day).
  • Certified Court Order Window: The certified court order serves as valid driving authorization for 45 days while DPS processes the physical ODL.
  • Strict Exclusions: ODLs cannot be issued for commercial motor vehicle operation. Operating outside court-stipulated hours or travel routes is a Class B misdemeanor resulting in immediate ODL revocation.

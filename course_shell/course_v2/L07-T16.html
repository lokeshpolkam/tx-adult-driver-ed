Texas Statutory Standards & Diagnostic Criteria

Qualifying Communication Impediments (37 TAC § 15.42)

Under Texas Administrative Code Title 37, Part 1, Chapter 15 (§ 15.42), the Texas Department of Public Safety authorizes Restriction Code 08 for drivers with diagnosed physical, sensory, or neurological conditions that can disrupt normal verbal communication during a traffic stop.

Diagnosed Condition Roadside Behavioral Profile Officer De-Escalation Mandate
Autism Spectrum (ASD) Delayed processing, lack of eye contact, literal speech interpretation, stimming under stress. Allow 10–15s latency; do not interpret lack of eye contact as guilt or hostility.
Speech Impediments Severe stuttering, dysarthria, apraxia, or situational vocal mutism. Do not interrupt or complete sentences; accept written notes or digital device prompts.
Severe Hearing Loss Inability to process verbal sirens or shouted roadside commands; lip-reading reliance. Face driver directly in illuminated area; use visual gestures and Texas visor cards.
PTSD / Traumatic Brain Injury Acute startle response, panic-induced freezing, hyper-vigilance during physical stops. Lower voice volume; announce physical movements before reaching near vehicle cabin.

Certifying Medical Authority Comparison

Texas law imposes strict distinctions between credential applications (DPS Form DL-101) and vehicle registration applications (TxDMV Form VTR-216):

Form DL-101 (DPS Credential)
PHYSICIANS ONLY

Must be signed by a licensed M.D. or D.O. Chiropractors, physical therapists, nurses, and psychologists are statutorily ineligible.

Form VTR-216 (TxDMV Plate)
EXPANDED ELIGIBILITY

Permits psychologists (Ph.D./Psy.D.) and licensed mental health professionals (LPC, LCSW, LMFT) for mental conditions under TTC § 502.061.

Confidentiality & $0.00 State Fee Mandate

Texas Transportation Code § 521.142(h) establishes two critical civil rights protections for all Texas motorists:

  • Zero Diagnostic Disclosure: The applicant’s clinical condition is never printed on the credential. Third parties inspecting the card (e.g. retail merchants, hotel clerks, or employers) cannot view medical history.
  • Discreet Restriction Code 08: Only the two-digit numeric code “08” appears under the restrictions column on the reverse face of the plastic card.
  • $0.00 State Fee: Texas DPS is statutorily prohibited from charging any diagnostic surcharge, renewal penalty, or registry fee for placing Code 08 on a driver license.

Submission Lifecycle & Practical Guidance

How to Tender Form DL-101 to Texas DPS:
  1. Initial License Issuance: Bring the physician-signed original Form DL-101 to your scheduled DPS appointment alongside Proof of Identity and Residency.
  2. Mid-Cycle Addition: If you already hold a valid Texas license, you do not need to wait until renewal. Schedule a “Change / Replacement” appointment, submit Form DL-101 and Form DL-43, and receive a reprinted card with Code 08.
  3. Removal / Opt-Out: Because participation is 100% voluntary, a driver may request the removal of Restriction Code 08 at any time during a license renewal or replacement transaction without presenting medical counter-proof.

WITHOUT RESTRICTION CODE 08 HIGH ESCALATION RISK

Encounter: Officer asks: “Do you know why I pulled you over?” The neurodivergent driver looks down, avoids eye contact, and remains silent for 12 seconds while rocking slightly.

Misinterpretation: The officer may suspect guilt, evasion, intoxication, or defiance, leading to heightened commands and potential command escalation.

WITH RESTRICTION CODE 08 PRESENTED TCOLE DE-ESCALATION RATIFIED

Encounter: The driver hands over their license. The officer inspects the card, spots “RESTRICTION CODE 08” on the back, and immediately adjusts approach.

Protocol Execution: The officer uses a calm, modulated voice, asks direct one-part questions (“Please look at this speed readout”), grants 15 seconds for cognitive processing, and accepts written responses without suspicion.

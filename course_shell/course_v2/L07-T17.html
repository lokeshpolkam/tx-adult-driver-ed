The Samuel Allen Law & Pre-Contact Strategy

Origins of Senate Bill 976 (86th Texas Legislature)

The Samuel Allen Law was named in honor of Samuel Allen, a young Texas driver with Asperger’s Syndrome who endured an intensely stressful law enforcement encounter when officers mistook his natural lack of eye contact and nervous vocal hesitations for intoxication and evasiveness.

To solve this problem statewide, the Texas Legislature enacted Texas Transportation Code § 502.061, linking communication impediment disclosures directly to the state vehicle registration database.

Family & Caregiver Vehicle Registration (43 TAC § 217.46)

A crucial provision of 43 TAC § 217.46 allows parents, spouses, and legal guardians to place the TLETS indicator on personal motor vehicles that are regularly operated by or used to transport a family member or dependent who has a certified communication impediment. The person with the medical condition does not need to own or hold title to the vehicle.

Zero External Markers & CJIS Data Safeguards

Under Texas law, the vehicle receives no exterior bumper stickers, window emblems, or special license plate symbols. Publicizing a disability on the vehicle exterior could make vulnerable drivers targets for predatory crime. The indicator exists exclusively in secure, encrypted law enforcement networks.

Cruiser Phase: Deputy runs plate TX J83-B921. MDT flashes priority advisory banner: “NOTICE: Communication Impediment — Approach with awareness.”

Approach Phase: The deputy dims high-beam spotlights, avoids barking urgent commands, and walks up to the driver door prepared for a 15-second response latency buffer.

Window Contact: The driver does not make eye contact and rocks slightly while reaching for insurance. Because the deputy was forewarned, zero escalation occurs, and the encounter concludes safely.

County Submission & Vehicle Sale Lifecycle

Administrative Steps under TxDMV Guidelines:
  1. Filing with County Tax Assessor: Submit executed Form VTR-216 (or Form VTR-29-CD) at your county tax assessor-collector’s office during annual registration renewal or via mail directly to TxDMV.
  2. Zero Registration Surcharge: Adding or removing the TLETS indicator is statutorily free ($0.00 state fee).
  3. Vehicle Transfer / Sale: If the vehicle is sold or title is transferred, the communication impediment marker remains tied to the owner’s registration record and is removed from the vehicle record when new license plates are issued to the buyer.

Texas Penal Code § 38.02 Safeguards (Failure to Identify)

Under Texas Penal Code § 38.02, a lawfully detained driver is required to provide their name, residence address, and date of birth to a peace officer upon request. For drivers with speech apraxia, autism-related sensory shutdown, or extreme auditory latency:

  • Document Tendering Satisfies Identification: Handing over an unexpired Texas Driver License or ID card physically fulfills the statutory requirement under § 38.02 without requiring instantaneous vocal fluency.
  • Protection Against False Refusal Charges: The active TLETS indicator establishes evidence in dispatch logs that the motorist has a recognized medical communication barrier, legally barring misinterpretation of delayed speech as criminal non-compliance.
  • Use of Alternative Augmentative Communication (AAC): Drivers may legally utilize smartphone speech synthesizers, communication boards, or typed messages to state their identifying details.

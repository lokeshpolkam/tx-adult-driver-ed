How to Participate in the Disability Program

Form VTR-29-CD Workflow • Medical Practitioner Certification • County Tax Assessor Submittal • DPS TLETS Integration

Texans participate in the landmark Texas Driving with Disabilities Program (The Samuel Allen Law) through two distinct statutory pathways: credential endorsement via Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) Form DL-101, and vehicle registration disclosure via Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (TxDMV) Form VTR-29-CD (formerly Form VTR-216). Under Texas Administrative Code Title 43, Rule § 217.46 and Texas Transportation Code § 502.061, participation is 100% voluntary, confidential, and completely free of additional state fees ($0.00). Submitting Form VTR-29-CD places a confidential electronic indicator into the Texas Law Enforcement Telecommunication System (TLETS), enabling peace officers to receive pre-contact notification on their in-car Mobile Data Terminals (MDT) prior to approaching the vehicle window during a roadside stop.

verified_user Texas DPS Statutory Rule:

Under 43 TAC § 217.46 and TTC § 502.061, submitting Form VTR-29-CD places a confidential communication impediment indicator in the Texas Law Enforcement Telecommunication System (TLETS). The indicator generates zero exterior license plate markings or windshield stickers. For physical health conditions, a licensed physician (MD/DO) must certify the form; for mental health conditions, a licensed physician, licensed psychologist, or licensed non-physician mental health professional (such as an LPC or LCSW) may certify.

shield Core Statutory Protections under 43 TAC § 217.46:

1. Multi-Vehicle Coverage: Parents, guardians, and spouses may file Form VTR-29-CD for all household vehicles regularly transporting the individual.
2. Anti-Stigma Privacy: The marker exists solely as an internal electronic flag in TLETS. Zero external decals or plate markings.
3. Voluntary Removal: Participation can be cancelled at any time by written request to TxDMV.

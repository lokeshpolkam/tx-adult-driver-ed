Responsibilities If Stopped by Law Enforcement

Safe Pull-Off Mechanics • Cabin Illumination • Document Protocols • Promise to Appear

Interactions between civilian motorists and Texas law enforcement are strictly codified under the Texas Transportation Code, the Texas Penal Code, and the Community Safety Education Act (Senate Bill 30). Under TTC § 545.156, a driver signaled by a patrol vehicle must immediately activate their right turn signal, yield the right-of-way, and safely bring the vehicle to a complete stop on the right shoulder or curb clear of any intersection. Crucially, signing a traffic citation under TTC § 543.005 is exclusively an administrative written promise to appear in court and explicitly is NOT an admission of guilt. Refusing to sign strips the officer of procedural discretion and requires an immediate custodial arrest. Willfully fleeing a police vehicle elevates instantly to a State Jail Felony under Texas Penal Code § 38.04.

PILLAR 01 • PULL-OVER DUTY TTC § 545.156 • § 545.421

Mandatory Yielding, Shoulder Berth & Evading Penalties

Under TTC § 545.156, upon the approach of an emergency vehicle utilizing audible and visual signals, the operator MUST immediately yield right-of-way, pull as close as practical to the right-hand curb or edge of the roadway, and stop clear of any intersection. Willfully failing or refusing to stop constitutes Fleeing or Attempting to Elude under TTC § 545.421 (Class B Misdemeanor, elevated to Class A if recklessly endangering others). Under TPC § 38.04, evading arrest in a motor vehicle is an automatic State Jail Felony (180 days to 2 years state jail, fine up to $10,000).

PILLAR 02 • COCKPIT DISCIPLINE SB 30 • TEX. EDUC. CODE § 28.012

Park Shift, Emergency Brake, Hazard Warning & Dome Light

Senate Bill 30 (Community Safety Education Act) codifies exact behavioral protocols inside the driver's cabin. Once stationary: (1) Shift transmission to Park; (2) Set the emergency parking brake; (3) Turn the engine OFF; (4) Activate 4-way hazard flashers; and (5) At night, immediately turn on the interior dome light to illuminate the cabin and eliminate shadows. Rolling down the driver window permits clear verbal communication. Exiting the vehicle on an active thoroughfare is strictly prohibited.

PILLAR 03 • CREDENTIALS & HANDS TTC § 521.025 • TPC § 38.02

Driver License Display Mandate & Zero Unprompted Reaching

Under TTC § 521.025 and § 601.053, drivers must possess and display their driver license and insurance on demand. Keep both hands anchored flat at 10 and 2 on the steering wheel until requested. Never reach into a glovebox, console, purse, or pocket before being asked. When requested, verbalize your movement in advance: "Officer, my insurance card is in the glovebox; I am reaching for it now." Under TPC § 38.02, intentionally giving false identity to an officer while lawfully detained is a Class B Misdemeanor.

PILLAR 04 • CITATION SIGNATURE TTC § 543.005 • § 543.001

Signature as Written Promise to Appear vs Mandatory Custodial Arrest

Under Texas Transportation Code § 543.005, signing a traffic citation is strictly an administrative written promise to appear in court on or before the specified date. It is explicitly NOT an admission of guilt and does not waive your right to contest the ticket in court. Refusing to sign removes the officer's discretion: the officer is statutorily required to effect an immediate custodial arrest, impound the motor vehicle, and physically transport the motorist before a magistrate.

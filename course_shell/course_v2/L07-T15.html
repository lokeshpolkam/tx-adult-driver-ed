SB 976 • THE SAMUEL ALLEN LAW 86TH LEGISLATURE

Legislative Foundation & Bridge to Law Enforcement

Enacted by the 86th Texas Legislature, SB 976 was created after Samuel Allen, an autistic Texan, faced stressful encounters during traffic stops due to misunderstood communication nuances. The law creates two proactive voluntary disclosure tracks: driver license restriction Code 08 (Form DL-101) and vehicle registration database indicators (Form VTR-29-CD).

37 TAC § 15.42 • DIAGNOSTIC CATEGORIES MEDICAL FIDELITY

Recognized Physical & Neurological Conditions

Under 37 TAC § 15.42, qualifying diagnoses include: Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD), Speech Impediments (stuttering, apraxia, dysarthria, mutism), Deafness or Severe Hearing Loss, Parkinson's Disease, Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI), and Intellectual/Developmental Disabilities (IDD).

SENSORY OVERLOAD • TRIGGER ANALYSIS NEURO-DIVERGENCE

Common Roadside Triggers vs. Erroneous Guilt Suspicions

• Averted Eye Contact: Neurological strategy to reduce auditory overstimulation, NOT deceit or evasiveness.
• Auditory Processing Latency: Autistic drivers often require 10 to 15 seconds to formulate spoken responses.
• Self-Soothing Movements: Rhythmic hand-flapping or rocking regulates sensory overload, NOT intoxication.
• Literal Interpretations: Drivers process language literally; figurative questions cause acute confusion.
STATUTORY SHIELD • CITIZEN LEGAL PROTECTIONS ADA TITLE II

Immunity Under TPC § 38.02 & Assistive Communication Rights

Drivers with certified communication impediments are legally protected from false charges under Texas Penal Code § 38.02 (Failure to Identify) when communication delays arise from a medical condition. Furthermore, under Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act (28 C.F.R. § 35.160), peace officers must permit drivers to utilize alternative communication aids—including written notes, visor cards, mobile texting, or digital AAC devices.

01 // CLINICAL AUTHENTICATION FORM DL-101

Physician Statement Mandate

The code is not self-selected at the DPS counter. An applicant must submit Texas DPS Form DL-101 signed by a licensed physician certifying a recognized medical condition that impedes oral dialogue.

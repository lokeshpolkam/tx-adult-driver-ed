PILLAR 01 • SEAT BELTS TTC § 545.413

Universal Safety Belt Standard & Driver Citation Liability

Texas Transportation Code § 545.413 mandates seat belt use for the driver and ALL passengers in front and rear seats. Adult violations (15+) carry fines of $25 to $50. Unbelted children under 17 carry fines of $100 to $200 assessed directly against the driver. Complete ejection from a vehicle increases collision fatality probability by 30-fold.

PILLAR 02 • CHILD RESTRAINTS TTC § 545.412

Child Safety Seat Mandate: Under 8 Years Unless > 4'9" Tall

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.412, children under 8 years of age must ride in an approved child safety seat or booster seat unless taller than 4 feet 9 inches (57 inches). Violations carry a misdemeanor fine of $25 to $250 to the driver. The 5-Step Fit Test governs safe transition to adult lap/shoulder belts.

PILLAR 03 • OPEN TRUCK BEDS TTC § 545.414 • § 545.415

Prohibition Against Transporting Minors Under 18 in Open Beds

Operating an open-bed truck or trailer with a passenger younger than 18 in the bed is a misdemeanor ($25 to $200 fine). Exactly 5 narrow defenses apply: parades, farm-to-market agricultural travel, beaches, sole family vehicle, and hayrides. Towed watercraft with minors are illegal under § 545.415 (sole family vehicle defense does NOT apply).

PILLAR 04 • 5-MINUTE RULE TPC § 22.10 • § 22.041

Leaving Child in Vehicle & Solar Greenhouse Hyperthermia

Texas Penal Code § 22.10 criminalizes leaving a child younger than 7 unattended in a vehicle for longer than 5 minutes without someone at least 14 (Class C Misdemeanor, $500 fine). Cabin heat rises 20°F in 10 minutes. Severe heat exposure escalates to State Jail Felony Child Endangerment (TPC § 22.041) or Criminally Negligent Homicide (TPC § 19.05).

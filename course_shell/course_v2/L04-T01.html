Yellow Clearance Interval & Dilemma Zone Threshold Braking

At 45 mph, a 4.2-second steady yellow clearance interval permits drivers unable to stop safely to clear the intersection. If stopping can be accomplished safely, the driver must apply threshold braking, stopping 3 feet before the marked stop bar before the 1.8-second all-red phase.

Mandatory Compliance Duty & Official Traffic Signal Indication Rules

Under Texas Transportation Code § 544.004, the operator of a vehicle shall comply with an official traffic-control device placed in accordance with state law unless directed otherwise by a police officer. Failure to obey is a Class C misdemeanor infraction carrying fines up to $200 and points on your Texas driver record.

traffic TTC § 544.007 SIGNAL INDICATION MATRIX
  • • Steady Red Signal (§ 544.007(d)): Operators must stop completely prior to the marked crosswalk or stop line. Right turn on red is permitted only AFTER a complete stop, yielding to all cross traffic and pedestrians, unless prohibited by an official sign.
  • • Steady Yellow Signal (§ 544.007(e)): Warns that the green movement is being terminated and a red indication will immediately exhibit. If you can stop safely before entering the intersection, you MUST stop; entering on yellow is legal only if stopping would cause a rear-end collision.
  • • Flashing Yellow Arrow (FYA - TMUTCD § 4D.17): Permissive left turn. You may cautiously enter the intersection, but you unconditionally must yield to oncoming traffic and pedestrians before executing the turn.
  • • Steady Green Arrow (§ 544.007(c)): Protected movement. Conflicting traffic is held by red signals. You have absolute right-of-way over all other movements.

PHASE: FLASHING YELLOW ARROW // PERMISSIVE TTC § 544.007(g)

Permissive Left Turn: Unconditional Yield Requirement

Under Texas Transportation Code § 544.007(g), an operator facing a flashing yellow arrow may cautiously enter the intersection to turn left, but MUST yield the right-of-way to oncoming vehicular traffic and pedestrians within the crosswalk. Oncoming traffic faces a steady green light and has absolute priority.

Full Stop Mandatory Prior to Stop Line or Crosswalk

The eight-sided octagon is reserved exclusively for STOP signs under Texas MUTCD and TTC § 544.010. Drivers must come to a complete operational cessation of vehicular motion before the marked stop line, crosswalk, or entering the intersecting roadway.

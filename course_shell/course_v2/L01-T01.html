Value of Adult Driver Education

Foundation for Reduced-Risk Driving & Lifetime Motorist Competency

Operating a motor vehicle on Texas public roads is a conditional legal privilege, not a constitutional right. Under Texas Transportation Code and TDLR Chapter 84, adult driver education establishes the cognitive, perceptual, and motor discipline required to eliminate kinetic chaos, prevent collisions, and protect vulnerable road users.

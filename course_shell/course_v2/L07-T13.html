Texas Driving with Disabilities Program

The Samuel Allen Law • Pre-Contact TLETS Telemetry • Code 08 Credential • Medical Privacy Safeguards

Traffic stops represent high-stakes, sensorially intense environments where rapid non-verbal movements or communication differences can be easily misinterpreted. Enacted by the 86th Texas Legislature as Senate Bill 976 (The Samuel Allen Law) and codified in Texas Transportation Code §§ 502.061 and 521.125, the Texas Driving with Disabilities Program establishes a groundbreaking voluntary framework allowing motorists with diagnosed communication impediments—such as Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), severe PTSD, speech apraxia, deafness, Parkinson's disease, or traumatic brain injury—to proactively notify law enforcement officers. By entering a private notification directly into the Texas Law Enforcement Telecommunication System (TLETS) or placing Restriction Code 08 on their driver license, citizens ensure peace officers receive crucial contextual awareness before approaching the vehicle, fostering informed, de-escalated, and safe interactions without public stigma or exterior vehicle decals.

The Samuel Allen Story & The Anatomy of Misunderstanding

The program owes its existence to Samuel Allen, a young autistic Texan who experienced repeated roadside stress and confusion during traffic encounters. Like many neurodivergent individuals, Samuel found flashing emergency strobe lights, loud public address commands, sudden physical proximity, and intense questioning sensorially overwhelming. Under severe stress, autistic drivers may avert direct eye contact, demonstrate delayed verbal response, exhibit motor stimming (such as hand flapping or rocking), or freeze completely.

In traditional law enforcement training without disability awareness, these behavioral manifestations are frequently misconstrued as deceit, illicit substance intoxication under Texas Penal Code § 49.01, active physical resistance, or criminal non-compliance under Texas Penal Code § 38.02 (Failure to Identify). In worst-case scenarios, rapid reaching for communication tools or sensory items can be misinterpreted as reaching for a weapon.

Recognizing this critical safety disconnect, the Texas Legislature enacted Senate Bill 976 with unanimous bipartisan support. The law establishes two mutually reinforcing pathways:

PATHWAY 1: CREDENTIAL FORM DL-101

Person-Tied Restriction Code

Submitted to Texas DPS. Places discreet Restriction Code 08 on the reverse face of the driver license or ID card. Follows the person regardless of what vehicle they drive or occupy.

PATHWAY 2: VEHICLE REGISTRY FORM VTR-29-CD

Pre-Contact TLETS MDT Alert

Submitted to County Tax Assessor. Ties an electronic communication impediment flag to the license plate. Alerts the officer inside the patrol unit before approaching the driver's window.

shield Anti-Stigma Privacy Architecture:

To protect participants from social stigmatization, public harassment, or predatory targeting, Texas law explicitly forbids placing decals, bumper stickers, or specialized plate characters on participating vehicles. The registration record is 100% electronic, strictly restricted to certified law enforcement terminals via TLETS.

Passed by the 86th Texas Legislature in 2019, the Samuel Allen Law enables voluntary, confidential notification of a communication impediment (such as autism, deafness, PTSD, or speech disorders) through the driver license credential or motor vehicle registration. PATHWAY 01 // TEXAS TRANSPORTATION CODE § 521.125 TEXAS DPS JURISDICTION

Driver License & State ID Credential Endorsement (Form DL-101)

The credential pathway links the communication impediment directly to the individual person. Regardless of whether the individual is operating their own vehicle, driving an employer's commercial van, renting a sedan, or riding as an identified passenger, handing their Texas Driver License to an officer provides statutory notice.

STEP 1: MEDICAL STATEMENT

Applicant submits Form DL-101 ("Physician Statement for Communication Impediment") completed and signed exclusively by a licensed physician (M.D. or D.O.).

STEP 2: DPS SUBMISSION

Presented in person at a Texas DPS Driver License office during original issuance, renewal, or replacement. No state diagnostic fee is assessed.

PATHWAY 02 // TEXAS TRANSPORTATION CODE § 502.061 TxDMV JURISDICTION

Vehicle Registration Record & TLETS Integration (Form VTR-29-CD)

The vehicle registration pathway links the notification directly to the motor vehicle. When an officer runs the license plate from inside their patrol cruiser during a stop, the system queries the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles registration database and alerts the officer via TLETS prior to stepping out of the cruiser.

STEP 1: DUAL PRACTITIONER SCOPE

Under 43 TAC § 217.46, mental health conditions can be signed by an M.D./D.O., licensed psychologist, or licensed non-physician mental health professional (LPC/LCSW). Physical conditions require an M.D./D.O.

STEP 2: COUNTY TAX ASSESSOR

Form VTR-29-CD (formerly VTR-216) is submitted to the local County Tax Assessor-Collector during initial registration or annual renewal. Can cover multiple family vehicles.

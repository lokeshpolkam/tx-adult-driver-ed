DPS Core Knowledge Review Pillars

Essential Texas Driver Handbook statutory rules required to score ≥ 70% on the final exam:

Pillar 1: Right-of-Way at Intersections (TTC § 545.151) CH. 4 STATUTES

Uncontrolled Intersections: Yield to any vehicle that entered the intersection before you. If two vehicles arrive simultaneously, the driver on the left must yield to the vehicle on the right.

T-Intersections: Vehicles traveling on the terminating road must yield right-of-way to all traffic on the through street.

Paved vs Unpaved: Drivers on unpaved roads must yield to all vehicles on paved roads.

Left Turns: Yield right-of-way to any vehicle approaching from the opposite direction that is within the intersection or so close as to constitute an immediate hazard.

Pillar 2: Signals, Signs & Pavement Markings CH. 5 STATUTES

Flashing Red Light: Treat as a stop sign. Come to a complete stop at the stop line, yield to approaching traffic, and proceed when safe.

Flashing Yellow Arrow: Left turns are permitted, but you must yield to oncoming traffic and pedestrians.

Double Solid Yellow Lines: No passing from either direction. Crossing is permitted only when making a lawful left turn into a driveway or private road.

Solid White Line: Indicates lane change is discouraged. Double solid white lines prohibit lane changes entirely.

Pillar 3: Signaling, Passing & Turning CH. 6 STATUTES

100-Foot Signal Mandate (TTC § 545.104): You must signal continuously for at least 100 feet before turning or changing lanes.

Passing on the Right: Permitted only when the vehicle being passed is making or preparing to make a left turn, or upon a one-way street or roadway with unobstructed pavement of sufficient width for two or more lanes. Never pass on the paved or unpaved shoulder.

Prima Facie Speed Limits (TTC § 545.352): 30 MPH in urban districts, 15 MPH in alleys, and 70 MPH on state/federal highways daytime (unless posted otherwise).

Pillar 4: Safety Restraints, DUI & Move Over Act CH. 8-10 STATUTES

Zero Tolerance for Minors: Any detectable amount of alcohol (0.00% BAC) for drivers under 21 constitutes a Class C misdemeanor DUI. Adult per se limit is 0.08% BAC.

Mandatory Seat Belts (TTC § 545.413): Universal mandate for all front and rear seat occupants. Children under 8 years must be secured in a child passenger safety seat system unless taller than 4 feet 9 inches.

Move Over or Slow Down (TTC § 545.157): Vacate lane closest to stationary emergency or TxDOT vehicles with flashing lights, or slow down to 20 MPH below the posted speed limit (or 5 MPH if posted under 25 MPH).

Pillar 5: Railroads, School Buses & Emergency Vehicles CH. 4 & 9 STATUTES

Railroad Grade Crossings (TTC § 545.251): Drivers approaching a railroad crossing must come to a complete stop between 15 feet and 50 feet from the nearest rail when warning signals flash, crossbuck arms lower, or an approaching train is visible or emitting an audible signal.

School Bus Flashing Red Lights (TTC § 545.066): Drivers approaching from either direction must stop prior to reaching a school bus displaying visual flashing red warning signals. You must remain stopped until the bus resumes motion, the driver signals you to proceed, or the visual signals are deactivated. On divided roadways with an unpaved median or physical barrier, opposing traffic is not required to stop.

Approaching Emergency Vehicles (TTC § 545.156): On the immediate approach of an authorized emergency vehicle using audible or visual signals, the operator shall yield the right-of-way, immediately drive to a position parallel to and as close as possible to the right-hand edge or curb of the roadway, and stop clear of any intersection until the emergency vehicle has safely passed.

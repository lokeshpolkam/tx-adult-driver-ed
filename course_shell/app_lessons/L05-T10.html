Vehicle Lights: Operation & Dimming Rules

Vehicle lighting serves a dual statutory function: revealing forward highway hazards and broadcasting vehicle presence to other road users. Texas Transportation Code Chapter 547 governs mandatory illumination hours, low-beam adverse weather usage, and statutory high-beam dimming distances.

Oncoming Traffic High Beam Dimming Mandate:

Under Texas Transportation Code § 547.333(c)(1), an operator approaching an oncoming vehicle within 500 feet must dim high-beam headlamps to low beams. High beams cast 350 to 500 feet of illumination, causing instant retinal saturation and flash blindness to approaching drivers.

TTC § 547.302 SUNSET / SUNRISE

30-Min Illumination Rule

Display lighted headlamps from 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise, and whenever atmospheric conditions reduce forward sightline below 1,000 feet.

Minimum Visibility 1,000 Feet Threshold
TTC § 547.333(c)(1) 500 FEET

Oncoming Vehicle Dimming

Whenever approaching an oncoming vehicle traveling in the opposite direction, switch from high beams to low beams prior to coming within 500 feet of the vehicle.

Oncoming Range ≥ 500 Ft Advance Dim
TTC § 547.333(c)(2) 300 FEET

Following Vehicle Dimming

When following behind another vehicle traveling in the same direction, you must dim your high beams within 300 feet to avoid reflecting blinding light through their rearview mirrors.

FOG & PRECIPITATION LOW BEAMS ONLY

Aerosol Glare Backscatter

Never use high beams in fog, heavy rain, or blowing snow. High beams shine directly into suspended droplets, reflecting blinding white glare back into your eyes. Low beams illuminate road surface below fog.

Lesson Synthesis

Statutory vehicle lighting prevents night collisions: display lamps from 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise (or whenever sight is < 1,000'), dim high beams at 500' oncoming and 300' trailing, and never use high beams in heavy fog.

balance CORE LEGAL TAKEAWAY

Under Texas Transportation Code § 547.302, driving with only parking lights on when headlamps are required is illegal. You must activate full low-beam headlights during dark twilight and low-visibility weather, and dim high beams within 500 feet of oncoming vehicles (§ 547.333).

STATUTORY AUTHORITY // SECTION 03

Device Jurisdiction & Legal Rules

4 PILLARS
PILLAR 01 // JURISDICTION account_balance

Authority to Place Devices

Under TTC § 544.002, only TxDOT on state highways and local municipal authorities on local streets possess legal power to place and maintain traffic control devices. Private imitation signs are illegal public nuisances.

TTC § 544.002 · EXCLUSIVE JURISDICTION
PILLAR 02 // DEFACEMENT dangerous

Device Damaging Penalties

Under TTC § 544.005, intentionally altering, defacing, knocking down, or removing an official traffic control device is a serious criminal offense punishable by Class B/A misdemeanor or felony penalties if personal injury results.

TTC § 544.005 · CRIMINAL SANCTIONS
PILLAR 03 // RED LIGHT VIOLATION traffic

Legal Definition of Red Light

Under TTC § 544.007, a red-light violation occurs strictly when a vehicle enters the intersection or crosses the stop line after the light has illuminated steady red. Being inside the intersection when yellow changes to red is lawful.

TTC § 544.007 · ENTRY THRESHOLD RULE
PILLAR 04 // CAMERA BAN videocam_off

Texas Red-Light Camera Ban

Texas Transportation Code § 707.020 (enacted via Texas HB 1631) banned photographic red-light enforcement cameras statewide. Traffic enforcement on Texas roadways must be conducted by certified law enforcement officers.

TTC § 707.020 · HB 1631 STATEWIDE BAN

Under Texas Transportation Code § 544.002 and § 544.004, official devices placed by TxDOT and local authorities establish legally binding operational limits, but defensive drivers never forfeit vigilance or assume other vehicles will yield.

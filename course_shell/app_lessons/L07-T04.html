Responsibilities of a Defensive Driver: IPDE Process

Defensive driving is the art of seeing hazards before they become emergencies. By systematically executing the four phases of the IPDE process—Identify, Predict, Decide, and Execute—drivers anticipate conflicts, maintain safe space cushions, and preserve escape corridors.

Defensive Driving Operational Network

Four critical tactical principles for scanning ahead, managing open escape corridors, and neutralizing reckless road users.

Phase 01 SCAN HORIZON

12-15 Second Visual Horizon

Never fixate only on the bumper ahead. Scan 12 to 15 seconds ahead in cities (1 to 2 blocks) and 20 to 30 seconds on highways to spot stalled traffic early.

HORIZON 15-30 SECONDS
Phase 02 BUFFER

The 360° Space Cushion

Maintain a generous buffer around all sides of your vehicle. Keep at least one adjacent lane or shoulder open as an emergency escape path if a forward pileup occurs.

CUSHION ESCAPE ROUTE READY
Phase 03 PLATOONS

Never Cruise in Packs

Vehicle clusters on highways eliminate reaction space. When surrounding cars pack closely together, ease off the accelerator or pass safely to find open road.

TACTIC SEEK OPEN POCKETS
Phase 04 TEMPERAMENT

Yielding Right-of-Way

Right-of-way is given, never taken. If another motorist fails to yield at an intersection or merge, yield willingly. Being legally right is meaningless in a crash.

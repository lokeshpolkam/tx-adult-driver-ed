Transporting Cargo, Safety Chains & Towing

Texas Transportation Code § 545.409 and § 725.021 mandate rigorous safety standards for towed trailers, drawbars, and loose cargo. Drivers must cross safety chains underneath the trailer tongue to cradle it upon decoupling, secure loose aggregate with tarps, and mark overhanging loads.

Crossed Safety Chains

Form an "X" beneath the tongue hitch. This catches the tongue before it impacts the roadway, maintaining tracking control if the hitch uncouples.

CRADLE // X-CONFIGURATION
02

4-Foot Overhang Rule

When cargo extends more than 4 feet beyond the rear body, attach a 12-inch red flag during daylight or a red light visible from 500 feet at night.

TTC § 547.382 // OVERHANG
03

Tarping Loose Bulk Loads

TTC § 725.021 requires that open beds transporting sand, dirt, rocks, or wood debris must be covered completely with canvas tarps to prevent roadway littering.

TTC § 725.021 // TARPING
04

Tow Bar 15-Foot Limit

Drawbars or ropes used to tow disabled vehicles cannot exceed 15 feet. A white cloth/flag must be secured to the center of the tow line for visibility.

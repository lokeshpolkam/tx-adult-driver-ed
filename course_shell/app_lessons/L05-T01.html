The Definition of Traffic Flow

Traffic flow is the collective, continuous movement of motor vehicles, pedestrians, and cyclists along shared roadways. Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.051 and § 545.351, optimal highway flow requires maintaining uniform cruising speeds, adequate following buffers, and predictable lane discipline.

Phantom Traffic Jams

When drivers tailgate, a single tap of the brakes forces the following car to brake harder. This reaction amplifies backward through the traffic platoon, creating gridlock miles behind with zero physical crash or obstruction.

PILLAR 03 // BASIC SPEED speed

Texas Basic Speed Rule

Under TTC § 545.351, driving at the posted speed limit is illegal if road or atmospheric conditions make that speed unsafe. Heavy congestion, rain, or fog legally requires reducing speed to what is reasonable and prudent.

TTC § 545.351 · PRUDENT SPEED MANDATE
PILLAR 04 // IMPEDING block

Impeding Traffic Prohibition

Under TTC § 545.363, an operator may not drive so slowly as to impede the normal and reasonable movement of traffic, except when reduced speed is necessary for safe operation. Driving abnormally slow creates dangerous passing conflicts.

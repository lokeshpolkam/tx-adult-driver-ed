Passing Stopped Service Vehicles

Texas Transportation Code § 545.157 establishes clear kinetic safeguards for emergency personnel and roadside workers. Drivers approaching stationary authorized emergency and service vehicles must vacate the adjacent lane or substantially decelerate.

APPROACH DRILL // SECTION 04

3-Stage Shoulder Approach Procedure

STAGE 01 ➔ 03
1 RECOGNIZE
Visual Scan

Spot flashing emergency or amber beacons at least 500 feet ahead; cancel cruise control.

2 VACATE
Lane Change

Signal left, check mirrors and blind spots, and shift smoothly into the adjacent left lane.

3 DECELERATE
Reduce Speed

If blocked from changing lanes, decelerate to at least 20 mph below limit (or 5 mph in <25 zones).

The Move Over or Slow Down Act protects roadway first responders and roadside crews. On multi-lane roads, vacate the adjacent lane. If unable to move over, slow down 20 mph below the posted limit (or to 5 mph in under-25 mph zones). Never trail fire apparatus closer than 500 feet.

Post-Collision Procedures & Crash Reporting Laws

Following a collision, immediate scene stabilization and statutory adherence prevent secondary crashes. Under Texas Transportation Code § 550.022 ("Steer It Clear It"), if an accident occurs on a main freeway lane with zero injuries and vehicles are drivable, drivers must move vehicles to the nearest shoulder, median, or exit ramp.

Texas "Steer It, Clear It" Law (TTC § 550.022):

If an accident occurs on a main lane, ramp, shoulder, or adjacent area of a freeway in a metropolitan area and each vehicle can be normally and safely driven, each operator shall move the vehicle as soon as possible to a designated accident investigation site, a location on the frontage road, or the nearest suitable shoulder to prevent catastrophic secondary collisions.

KEY STATUTORY TAKEAWAY

Under Texas Transportation Code § 550.022 ("Steer It Clear It"), motorists in non-injury collisions with drivable vehicles must clear freeway travel lanes onto the nearest shoulder. An official Texas Peace Officer's Crash Report (Form CR-3) is legally required for crashes involving injury, death, or $1,000 or more in apparent property damage.

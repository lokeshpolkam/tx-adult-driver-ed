TDLR MANDATE · VULNERABLE ROAD USER STATUTE

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.428, vulnerable road users include pedestrians, bicyclists, motorcyclists, road construction workers, and operators of non-motorized vehicles. Drivers must exercise due care to avoid collisions.

Multi-Modal Roadway Dynamics

Four critical operational dynamics for interacting safely with distinct user categories on Texas streets and highways.

Dynamic 01 TTC § 552.003

Crosswalk Right-of-Way

Drivers must yield when a pedestrian is on the half of the roadway the vehicle is traveling or approaching so closely as to be in danger. Never pass a vehicle stopped at a crosswalk.

RULE YIELD & STOP
Dynamic 02 CONSPICUITY

Motorcycle Conspicuity

Motorcycles have narrow profiles easily concealed by window pillars and blind spots. Over 50% of multi-vehicle motorcycle crashes occur at intersections when cars turn left across their path.

CHECK DOUBLE LOOK LEFT
Dynamic 03 TTC § 547.008

Slow-Moving Vehicles (SMV)

Farm tractors and construction equipment operate at 25 mph or less and display a fluorescent orange triangle with retroreflective red border. Reduce speed immediately upon sighting.

SPEED <= 25 MPH DESIGN
Dynamic 04 WORK ZONES

Road Construction Crews

Fines double in Texas maintenance and construction work zones when workers are present. Obey flaggers, maintain buffer lanes, and eliminate all phone distractions.

PENALTY FINES DOUBLED

balance Core Legal Takeaway

Vulnerable road users lack the structural protection of passenger vehicles. Texas law requires drivers to exercise extraordinary care, yield right-of-way at crosswalks, come to a complete stop for pedestrians with white canes or guide dogs, and give ample passing clearance to cyclists and motorcyclists.

Wireless Devices & Distracted Driving Statutes

Texas strictly penalizes portable electronic device usage behind the wheel. Reading, typing, or sending an electronic message while driving is illegal statewide under Texas Transportation Code § 545.4251. Glancing down at a mobile phone for just 5 seconds at 55 MPH means traveling over 400 feet completely blind.

Statewide Texting Ban

TTC § 545.4251 makes it a misdemeanor offense to read, write, or send texts, emails, or messages while driving. Fines range from $25 to $99 for first offenses, and up to $200 for subsequent convictions.

02

School Crossing Zones

TTC § 545.425 prohibits any handheld wireless phone usage within active school zones while flashing beacons operate or speed limits are reduced. Fines double up to $200.

03

Drivers Under 18 Years

TTC § 545.424 establishes an absolute prohibition: teens holding a learner permit or provisional license are forbidden from using ANY wireless device, including hands-free Bluetooth systems.

KEY STATUTORY TAKEAWAY

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.4251, reading, writing, or sending an electronic message while driving is illegal across all Texas public roadways. Handheld phones are strictly prohibited in active school zones, and drivers under 18 may not use wireless devices under any circumstances.

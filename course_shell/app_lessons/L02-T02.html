directions_car CLASS C: Standard Motor Vehicle Operator
TTC § 521.083
1. Weight Cap

Single vehicles up to 26,000 lbs GVWR (passenger cars, SUVs, and pickups).

2. Towing Rating

Can tow trailers up to 10,000 lbs; farm trailers up to 20,000 lbs.

3. Exclusions

Excludes 16+ passenger vans, commercial freight chassis, and hazardous cargo.

CODE 'A' // RESTRICTION TTC § 521.221

Corrective Lenses

Attached when applicant requires glasses or contact lenses to achieve 20/40 visual acuity. Driving without corrective lenses is a misdemeanor.

CONDITION Must Wear While Driving
CODE 'B' // RESTRICTION TTC § 521.222

LOFS 21+ Accompaniment

Licensed Operator Front Seat. Attached to learner permits. Mandatory licensed adult 21+ with 1+ year driving experience in front passenger seat.

SUPERVISION Adult 21+ Required
SPECIAL // ENDORSEMENT TTC § 521.125

Communication Impediment

Voluntary notation for autism, deaf/hard-of-hearing, or speech conditions. Alerts law enforcement via TLETS system during traffic stops.

TLETS Officer Pre-Alert
EXEMPTION // RESTRICTED TTC § 521.223

verified CORE PRINCIPLE // RESTRICTION & VEHICLE DISCIPLINE

Under TTC § 521.083, a standard Class C license permits operating vehicles up to 26,000 lbs GVWR and towing trailers up to 10,000 lbs. Motorcycles strictly require a Class M endorsement (§ 521.084). Operating out of restriction constitutes a misdemeanor under § 521.221.

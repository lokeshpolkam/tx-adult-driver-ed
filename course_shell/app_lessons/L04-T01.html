Signs, Signals, and Markings

Traffic control devices establish legal priority and communicate roadway conditions before physical geometry changes. Texas Transportation Code § 544.004 mandates strict compliance with all official traffic signals, regulatory signs, and pavement markings under Texas MUTCD standards.

Sign Silhouette Grammar

8 SIDES // RED pan_tool

Octagon — Full Stop

Exclusively reserved for STOP signs under MUTCD R1-1. The unique 8-sided silhouette allows recognition from the rear or when covered by dirt/snow.

ACTION: COMPLETE STOP TTC § 544.010
DOWNWARD TRIANGLE change_history

Equilateral Triangle — Yield

Exclusively reserved for YIELD signs (MUTCD R1-2). Commands drivers to decelerate and surrender right-of-way to all vehicles in the conflict stream.

ACTION: YIELD RIGHT-OF-WAY TTC § 545.151
DIAMOND // YELLOW warning

Diamond — Hazard Warning

Alerts operators to upcoming physical hazards, sharp curves, pedestrian crossings, or slippery surfaces. Orange diamond indicates work zones.

ACTION: REDUCE SPEED MUTCD CH. 2C
PENTAGON // GREEN-YELLOW school

Pentagon — School Zone

Exclusively designates public school zones and school crosswalks. Features fluorescent yellow-green coating and triggers speed drop to 20 mph.

ACTION: 20 MPH SPEED LIMIT MUTCD S1-1

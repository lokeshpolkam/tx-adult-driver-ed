Rear-Wheel Skid Recovery Protocol:

When the rear wheels lose traction and break loose, instantly ease your foot completely off the accelerator pedal. Never touch the brakes. Turn the steering wheel smoothly in the direction the rear of the car is skidding (“steer into the skid”). As the vehicle straightens, smoothly counter-steer to prevent secondary fishtailing.

Emergency Malfunction Tactical Profiles

Four critical highway mechanical failures and the counter-intuitive driver techniques required to survive them.

Emergency 01 OVERSTEER

Rear-Wheel Skid

When the rear slides, ease off the accelerator immediately. Never slam brakes. Steer smoothly into the direction of the skid (the direction the rear end is sliding) until grip returns.

RULE STEER INTO SKID
Emergency 02 BLOWOUT

Tire Blowout at 60 MPH

Car will pull violently toward the blown tire. Grip steering wheel firmly with both hands, do not brake abruptly, ease off throttle, and coast to a gentle stop on the shoulder.

GRIP BOTH HANDS FIRM
Emergency 03 BRAKE FAILURE

Hydraulic Brake Failure

If the pedal sinks to the floorboards: rapidly pump the pedal to rebuild hydraulic fluid pressure, shift transmission down into lower gear, and gradually apply the parking brake.

ACTION PUMP & DOWNSHIFT
Emergency 04 OFF-ROAD

Low Shoulder Drop-Off

If right tires drop off paved asphalt onto gravel, do NOT jerk the wheel back left. Hold wheel straight, ease off gas, decelerate, check mirrors, and steer smoothly back at a gentle angle.

RULE SLOW BEFORE RETURNING

QUESTION 1 OF 1 TX DRIVER HANDBOOK CH. 5

If the rear wheels of your vehicle lose traction and skid to the right, how should you steer to recover?

check_circle CORRECT ANSWER

Statutory Precept: Under Texas driver safety regulations and vehicle dynamics, when recovering from a rear-wheel skid, ease off the gas, do not slam brakes, and steer in the direction of the skid (the direction the rear is sliding) to realign front and rear wheels.

Lesson Synthesis

Surviving highway vehicle emergencies requires overriding panic reflexes: steer into skids without braking, grip blowouts firmly with both hands, pump failed brakes while downshifting, and slow down before returning from low shoulders.

balance Core Legal Takeaway

Texas Transportation Code § 545.401 imposes liability on drivers whose improper emergency responses result in loss of control or collisions. In skids, steering into the skid realigns the tires; slamming on the brakes breaks whatever remaining grip exists. In tire blowouts, maintain a straight line and coast to a stop.

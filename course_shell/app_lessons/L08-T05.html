TTC § 545.420 (Racing on Highway): A person may not participate in a race, vehicle speed competition, drag race, or exhibition of vehicle speed on a Texas public highway. Violations trigger immediate vehicle seizure and impoundment.

1st Offense Street Racing

Class B Misdemeanor punishable by up to 180 days in jail and up to a $2,000 fine. Law enforcement conducts mandatory vehicle seizure and impoundment at owner expense.

CLASS B // MANDATORY IMPOUND

Repeat Offense or Open Container

Class A Misdemeanor with mandatory minimum 10-day jail confinement and fines up to $4,000 if the driver had an open alcoholic beverage container or prior racing conviction.

03

Injury & Fatal Collisions

Third-degree felony for bodily injury. If serious bodily injury or death results, it escalates to a Second-Degree Felony punishable by 2 to 20 years in prison and up to $10,000 fine.

FELONY // 2 TO 20 YRS STATE PRISON

Spectators & Organizers

Individuals who coordinate street takeovers, block intersections, or attend illegal racing exhibitions can be criminally charged with Class B or Class A misdemeanors.

Distractions That Affect Driving — Tech, Pets & Food

Driver distraction is an epidemic responsible for thousands of catastrophic highway collisions annually. Highway safety science categorizes distraction into three distinct types: Visual, Manual, and Cognitive. Operating a vehicle demands 100% of your cognitive attention, two hands, and forward visual focus.

The 5-Second Blind Horizon (TTC § 545.4251):

At 55 MPH, taking your eyes off the road for an average text (5.0 seconds) means driving blind for 403 feet—more than the length of an entire football field with end zones included. Texas law prohibits reading, writing, or sending electronic messages while operating a motor vehicle.

KEY STATUTORY TAKEAWAY

Under NHTSA and TDLR driver safety standards, distractions are categorized into three core types: Visual (looking away), Manual (removing hands from controls), and Cognitive (mental focus diverted). Hands-free devices do not eliminate cognitive distraction, and Texas law strictly bans texting while driving.

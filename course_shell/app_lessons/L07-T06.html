Responsibilities If Stopped by Law Enforcement

Texas law and the Community Safety Education Act (Senate Bill 30) define standard operating protocols for traffic stops. Understanding how to pull over safely, maintain calm communication, keep hands visible, and retrieve credentials ensures safety and transparency for both citizens and law enforcement officers.

Community Safety Education Act / Senate Bill 30 Protocol:

Under Texas Senate Bill 30 and Texas Transportation Code § 545.421, when an officer activates emergency overhead lights or siren behind you, immediately signal right, slow down, and safely maneuver completely onto the right-side roadway shoulder. Put the transmission in PARK, set the emergency brake, turn off the engine, turn on the interior dome light at night, roll down the window, and keep both hands visibly placed on the steering wheel.

balance Core Legal Takeaway

When stopped by law enforcement, pull safely to the right shoulder, place the vehicle in park, turn on interior lights at night, and keep hands visible on the steering wheel. Notify the officer before reaching for credentials. Signing a citation is merely a promise to appear, not an admission of guilt.

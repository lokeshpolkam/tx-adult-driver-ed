Fatigue Warning Signs & Clinical Countermeasures

Four critical diagnostic indicators of driving impairment and the mandated protocols to avert catastrophe.

Indicator 01 PHYSICAL SYMPTOM

Heavy Eyelids & Blinking

Involuntary eyelid fluttering, slow pupil readjustment, and systemic inability to maintain stable optical focal points indicate pre-sleep neurological state.

ACTION EXIT IMMEDIATELY
Indicator 02 COGNITIVE TRANCE

Fixed Gaze (Hypnosis)

Staring blankly at center roadway lines without dynamic 12–15 second visual scanning. Peripheral hazard perception drops by 60% and memory encoding stops.

Indicator 03 CRITICAL HAZARD

Rumble Strip Vibration

Hitting shoulder rumble strips is an unmistakable warning that you have suffered a microsleep. Sudden steering over-corrections frequently cause rollovers.

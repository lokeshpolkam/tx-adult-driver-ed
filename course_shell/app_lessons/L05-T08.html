TADD PROTOCOL 12–24 INCHES

Turn Around, Don't Drown

Moving water is deceptive. 6 inches reaches car bottoms and causes stalling. 12 to 24 inches provides sufficient buoyancy to float passenger cars, SUVs, and heavy pickup trucks downstream.

TTC § 472.022 CLASS B CRIME

Driving Past Barricades

Disobeying a warning sign or driving around barricades on a flooded road is a Class B Misdemeanor punishable by fines up to $1,000, up to 180 days in jail, and liability for emergency rescue costs.

Maximum Penalty $1,000 + 180 Days Jail
RECOVERY ACTION EASE OFF GAS

Hydroplaning Recovery

If tires hydroplane, immediately ease your foot off the accelerator. Do not touch the brakes, do not turn the wheel abruptly. Keep tires pointed straight until rubber slices through water film.

Crucial Rule Never Slam Brakes
OIL SLICK EFFECT FIRST 30 MIN

Initial Rain Slick Hazard

Highways are most dangerous during the first 20 to 30 minutes of rain. Water mixes with dripped engine oil, transmission fluid, and rubber dust, forming a greasy slick until heavy rain washes it off.

Lesson Synthesis

Never underestimate flowing water or rain slicks. 12 to 24 inches of water can float and sweep away vehicles, hydroplaning can occur at speeds as low as 35 mph, and driving around barricades is a punishable criminal offense under TTC § 472.022.

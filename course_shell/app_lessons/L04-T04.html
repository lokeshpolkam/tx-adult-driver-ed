SCENARIO 01 // YELLOW traffic

Dilemma Zone Evaluation

Under TTC § 544.007(e), yellow light informs the operator that red is imminent. Stop safely before entering the intersection; if past the point of safe stopping, proceed through with caution. Never accelerate into the yellow.

TTC § 544.007(e) · CLEARANCE DUTY
SCENARIO 02 // BUS directions_bus

School Bus Stopping Mandate

Under TTC § 545.066, all vehicles approaching a stopped school bus operating alternating red flashing signals must come to a complete stop. Traffic in both directions must remain stopped until the bus moves or visual signals deactivate.

TTC § 545.066 · UP TO $1,250 FINE
SCENARIO 03 // WORK ZONE engineering

Highway Work Zones

Under TTC § 542.404, traffic fines are doubled in Texas highway work zones when construction workers are present. Drivers must slow down, merge early, and obey posted regulatory speed limits and orange guide signs.

TTC § 542.404 · FINES DOUBLED
SCENARIO 04 // OBSCURED visibility

Obscured Roadway Signs

When a stop or yield sign is obscured by foliage or parked trucks, safe drivers recognize secondary intersection cues: stop bars, curb returns, and crosswalks. Never assume an uncontrolled right-of-way when visibility is compromised.

CORE PRINCIPLE: Defensive driving demands that drivers look beyond the immediate hood of their vehicle. By scanning 12 to 15 seconds ahead, respecting yellow clearance transitions, stopping unconditionally for school buses, and decelerating through construction work zones, drivers proactively prevent collisions before physical emergencies arise.

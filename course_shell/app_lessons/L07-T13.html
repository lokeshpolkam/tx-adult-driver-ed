Driving with Disabilities Curriculum

The Texas Driving with Disabilities Program, created through the Samuel Allen Law (TTC § 502.061 and § 521.125), ensures safe, compassionate traffic stops for drivers with communication impediments. Participation is 100% voluntary, allowing motorists to privately link diagnoses to their driver license and state law enforcement computers (TLETS).

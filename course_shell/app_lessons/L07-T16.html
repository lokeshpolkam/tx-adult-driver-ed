Physical Disability vs Communication Impediment

Texas law strictly differentiates between physical mobility disabilities (TTC Chapter 681), which grant accessible parking placards, and communication impediments (TTC § 502.061), which facilitate peaceful law enforcement interactions. Having a communication impediment does NOT grant disabled parking privileges.

KEY STATUTORY TAKEAWAY

Under Texas Transportation Code § 502.061 and § 681.001, communication impediment registration assists officers during stops but confers zero accessible parking privileges. Parking in a disabled space or access aisle without an authentic physician mobility placard (Form VTR-214) is a Class C misdemeanor punishable by fines up to $1,250 and community service.

Defensive Driving: Anticipate & Respond
TTC § 545.061
MODULE 03 · TOPIC 07 TTC § 545.061 & § 545.152 verified TDLR MANDATE

Observe. Anticipate. Respond.

Defensive driving is the proactive discipline of identifying hazards before they materialize. Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.061 and § 545.401, having legal priority does not excuse driving into a collision. Real roadcraft means voluntarily conceding space to eliminate kinetic risk.

Texas Multi-Lane Merge Rule: Under TTC § 545.061, an operator entering a lane from the right shall yield the right-of-way to an operator entering the same lane from the left. Left-lane traffic travels in a higher-speed corridor and possesses statutory precedence.

3-Lane Highway Pinch

When entering the center lane simultaneously, the right-lane driver must yield. Cancel your blinker, hold position, and merge only after the left vehicle settles.

RULE: RIGHT YIELDS TTC § 545.061

Stranded Crosswalk Pedestrian

Even on a solid green signal, TTC § 544.007(b) mandates yielding to pedestrians lawfully in the intersection. Never accelerate until the crosswalk is 100% clear.

RULE: HUMAN LIFE FIRST TTC § 552.008

Stale Green Defense

A light that has been green for an extended distance will change. Cover the brake pedal, identify your point of no return, and prepare for a safe stop before the yellow.

Aggressive Driver Steal

When an erratic driver pushes through your turn sequence or runs a stop bar, surrender the right-of-way calmly. Never honk angrily or retaliate with kinetic force.

Right-of-Way: Given, Not Taken

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.151, right-of-way is not an absolute personal right to take; it is a statutory duty to yield. Texas law never gives anyone the absolute right-of-way, but strictly designates who must surrender it to prevent collisions.

verified CORE PRINCIPLE // GIVEN, NEVER TAKEN

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.151, no motorist has an absolute legal right to proceed. The law only establishes who has the duty to yield. If another driver fails to yield, you must surrender right-of-way to avoid a collision. At uncontrolled intersections, the vehicle on the left yields to the vehicle on the right.

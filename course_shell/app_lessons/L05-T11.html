TDLR MANDATE · TTC § 545.154 & § 545.051 COMPLIANT

Under Texas law, vehicles entering a freeway from an on-ramp or feeder road must yield right-of-way to circulating freeway traffic. Vehicles exiting to an access road take right-of-way over access road traffic. Left lane is reserved for passing where designated.

Weaving Lane Priority

In shared auxiliary lanes where vehicles enter and exit simultaneously, the exiting vehicle has right-of-way over entering traffic because exiting cars are decelerating from high speeds.

QUESTION 1 OF 1 TTC § 545.154

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.154, what is the right-of-way rule for entering a controlled-access freeway?

check_circle CORRECT ANSWER

Statutory Precept: Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.154, an operator on an entrance ramp or feeder road to a freeway must yield right-of-way to vehicles traveling on the main freeway travel lanes.

Lesson Synthesis

Freeway operation demands kinetic synchronization on entry ramps, disciplined 3-4 second spatial cushions during cruising, passing-only usage of the left lane, and decelerating exclusively within off-ramp chambers.

balance Core Legal Takeaway

Entering traffic possesses zero automatic right-of-way over freeway mainlanes (TTC § 545.154). When exiting, maintain cruising speed until completely within the deceleration ramp taper. If an exit is missed, reversing is strictly prohibited under TTC § 545.415; you must proceed to the subsequent interchange.

Statutory Enhancement Architecture

Four critical criminal statutes governing DWI liability, felony upgrades, and sentencing enhancements in Texas courts.

Statute 01 § 49.04

First Offense DWI

Class B misdemeanor. Carries a fine up to $2,000, 72 hours to 180 days jail confinement, and license suspension up to 1 year. If an open container is found, minimum jail jumps to 6 days.

JAIL 72 HRS - 180 DAYS JAIL
Statute 02 § 49.04(d)

High BAC >= 0.15

If forensic testing reveals a BAC of 0.15 or greater, the crime escalates to a Class A misdemeanor: fine up to $4,000, up to 1 full year in county jail, and mandatory ignition interlock device (IID).

UPGRADE CLASS A / $4,000 FINE
Statute 03 § 49.045

Child Passenger Felony

Operating intoxicated with any passenger under 15 years old constitutes a State Jail Felony, regardless of prior clean record. Punishable by up to $10,000 fine and 180 days to 2 years in state jail.

LEVEL STATE JAIL FELONY
Statute 04 § 49.08

Intoxication Manslaughter

Causing the death of another person while operating intoxicated is a Second-Degree Felony. Conviction mandates 2 to 20 years in the Texas Department of Criminal Justice prison system and up to $10,000 fine.

PRISON 2 TO 20 YEARS TDCJ

What are the statutory criminal penalties for a first-offense adult DWI conviction in Texas?

check_circle CORRECT ANSWER

Statutory Precept: Under Texas Penal Code § 49.04, a first DWI offense is a Class B misdemeanor punishable by a criminal fine up to $2,000, 72 hours to 180 days jail confinement, and driver license suspension.

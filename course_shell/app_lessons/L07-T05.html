Occupant Restraint & Safety Network

Four critical operational dynamics for protecting passengers, securing children, and preventing vehicular heatstroke deaths.

Statute 01 TTC § 545.413

Universal Restraint Law

Every passenger must wear a seat belt regardless of whether they sit in the front or back row. Unbelted rear passengers become lethal human projectiles in a frontal crash.

RULE ALL ROWS BUCKLED
Statute 02 TTC § 545.412

Child Safety Seats

Children must use an approved child restraint system until age 8 or 4 feet 9 inches in height. Seat belts alone do not properly fit child pelvises and can cause internal organ trauma.

THRESHOLD AGE 8 OR 4'9"
Statute 03 TTC § 545.414

Open Truck Bed Ban

It is unlawful to operate an open-bed pickup truck or trailer with a passenger younger than 18 years in the bed. Sudden swerves or braking can eject occupants with fatal force.

PROHIBITION NO MINORS IN BED
Statute 04 TPC § 22.10

Hot Car Heatstroke

Leaving a child under 7 unattended for >5 mins is a crime. On an 80°F day, a car cabin reaches 100°F in 10 minutes and 125°F in 40 minutes. Children overheat 3 to 5 times faster than adults.

DANGER RAPID HYPERTHERMIA

balance Core Legal Takeaway

Texas law enforces universal seat belt usage for all vehicle occupants in all seating rows. Children under 8 must be secured in safety seats unless taller than 4'9", minors under 18 may never ride in open pickup truck beds, and leaving a child under 7 unattended for over 5 minutes is a crime.

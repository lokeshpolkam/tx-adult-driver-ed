PROGRESSIVE AUTHORIZATION // SECTION 03

The Graduated Driver License (GDL) Program

Texas enacted the GDL program to reduce crashes among young drivers by phasing in full privileges as novice operators accumulate highway hours.

1 PHASE ONE

Learner License

Instruction Permit • Ages 15–17

  • Requires 32 classroom hours driver ed
  • Accompanied by licensed adult 21+ in front
  • Must hold minimum 6 months uninterrupted
2 PHASE TWO

Provisional License

Vertical Format Card • Ages 16–17

  • Practical DPS Behind-the-Wheel test passed
  • Completed 44 behind-the-wheel hours (10 night)
  • Bound by the 3 Hard Statutory Restrictions
3 FULL PRIVILEGE

Class C Unrestricted

Horizontal Format • Age 18+

  • GDL restrictions expire automatically at 18
  • Full passenger & late-night corridor privileges
  • Renews every 6–8 years under standard statute
INTERLOCK RULE: Conviction of any moving violation during Phase One resets the 6-month holding requirement. TTC § 521.2711

SPECIAL STATUTORY DESIGNATION // HEALTH & SAFETY CODE CH. 692A & TTC § 521.401

Anatomical Gifts & The Glenda Dawson Registry

favorite GLENDA DAWSON DONATE LIFE-TEXAS
verified TEXAS ANATOMICAL GIFT ACT STATUTORY PRECEPT

In 2005, the 79th Texas Legislature enacted the Texas Anatomical Gift Act (Texas Health and Safety Code Chapter 692A) and created the official statewide registry in honor of State Representative Glenda Dawson, an organ recipient and champion of donation awareness. Under Texas Transportation Code § 521.401, Texas driver license applicants are provided an affirmative opportunity to register as organ, eye, and tissue donors.

Lifesaving Clinical Impact: A single registered organ donor can save up to 8 lives through vital organ transplantation (heart, 2 lungs, liver, 2 kidneys, pancreas, intestines) and enhance the lives of 75+ more through tissue and corneal transplants. Over 10,000 Texans are currently on the active organ waiting list.
Irrevocable Legal Precept (HSC § 692A.008): Under Texas law, an affirmative registration by an individual who is at least 18 years of age is a legally binding document of gift. Family members, next of kin, or executors CANNOT legally override, modify, or revoke an adult's registered donor designation upon death.

STATUTORY PENALTIES & REMEDIES // SECTION 06

Breach of License Authority & Legal Recourse

Driving on Texas public roads is a legally granted privilege, not an inherent constitutional right. The Texas Transportation Code imposes severe civil and criminal penalties for unlawful operation.

INFRACTION 01 TTC § 521.021

Driving Without a License

Operating without having been issued a valid credential, or failing to carry it on person while operating.

PENALTY PROFILE
  • Fine up to $200 (first offense)
  • Charge dismissed with $10 administrative fee if valid license produced in court before first hearing
  • 3rd offense within 1 year: Class B misdemeanor
CRIMINAL VIOLATION 02 TTC § 521.457

Driving While License Invalid (DWLI)

Operating a motor vehicle after a driver license has been canceled, suspended, revoked, or denied.

CRIMINAL CLASSIFICATION
  • Class B misdemeanor: Up to $2,000 fine & 180 days jail
  • Enhanced to Class A misdemeanor if involved in a crash causing serious bodily injury or death
  • Automatic doubling of the suspension period
STATUTORY REMEDY TTC § 521.242

Occupational Driver License (ODL)

Special restricted license issued by court order for an individual whose license is suspended, strictly for essential occupational or educational transit.

RESTRICTIVE CONDITIONS
  • Petition filed in county or district court
  • Mandatory proof of SR-22 financial responsibility
  • Operation capped at 4 hrs/day (or 12 hrs by court waiver)
  • Driver must maintain strict written logbook in car

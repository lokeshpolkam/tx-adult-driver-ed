The Definition of Traffic Flow

Traffic flow is the living movement of vehicles, pedestrians, and ridden animals on public roadways. It relies on a delicate balance of laws and cooperation.

The Living Traffic System

!

1. Signal Intentions

Predictability is the foundation of flow. Signaling allows other drivers in the system to adjust their speed smoothly without harsh braking.

2. Yield Right-of-Way

Yielding prevents intersection gridlock. Forcing the right-of-way disrupts cross-traffic and cascades delays throughout the network.

3. Maintain Distance

Following distances act as shock absorbers for the system. They absorb minor speed variations without forcing the entire lane to stop.

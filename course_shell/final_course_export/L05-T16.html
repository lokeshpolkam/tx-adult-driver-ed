360° Space Cushion Doctrine (TTC § 545.062):

Texas Transportation Code § 545.062 requires drivers to maintain an assured clear distance between vehicles. A complete 360-degree cushion protects the front (minimum 3–4 seconds), trailing rear, and both lateral flanks. Never travel in an adjacent vehicle's blind spot; stagger lane positions so you always hold at least one open evasive escape corridor.

Dynamic Space Cushion

TTC § 545.062

Maintain a non-negotiable 3–4 second baseline interval under dry, daytime conditions. Texas statute requires expanding this buffer to 6–8 seconds during precipitation, fog, or nighttime high-speed transit.

Continuous Intent Signaling

TTC § 545.104

An operator must continuously indicate an intention to turn or change lanes for not less than the last 100 feet traveled immediately prior to the maneuver. Tap signals do not meet legal compliance.

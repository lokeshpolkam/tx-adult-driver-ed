The Lethal Reality: 26% of Traffic Fatalities

In Texas, alcohol-related collisions account for over 26% of all statewide traffic fatalities. More than 1,000 lives are ended prematurely every year on Texas thoroughfares solely due to preventable impaired driving decisions.

Teen & Novice Risk: Nearly 25% Drinking in Fatal Crashes

National and Texas safety statistics reveal that nearly 25% of teenage drivers involved in fatal collisions had been consuming alcohol. For drivers under 21, Texas enforces an unforgiving absolute Zero Tolerance standard.

The Golden Rule: Zero Tolerance & Defensive Duty

Never mix drugs, never drink and drive, and always consult a physician or pharmacist before operating a motor vehicle on prescription or over-the-counter medication. Remember: Texas statute § 49.10 explicitly bars legal prescription ownership as a defense against DWI.

• L01-T03 · PRIVILEGE & CIVIC OBLIGATION | TEXAS TRANSPORTATION CODE // CHAPTER 521

Driving is a privilege, not a constitutional right

The operation of a motor vehicle on Texas public roadways is a conditional privilege granted by the State, governed by statutory covenants, strict civil liability, and an enduring civic obligation to fellow roadway users.

CIVIC COVENANT // SECTION 04

The Six Core Driver Obligations

Holding a Texas driver license creates an affirmative duty across six statutory pillars.

BODILY INJURY / PERSON $30,000
BODILY INJURY / CRASH TOTAL $60,000
PROPERTY DAMAGE / CRASH $25,000

Texas Safety Responsibility Act (§ 601.051). Mandatory minimum liability coverage: $30,000 injury/death per person, $60,000 total per crash, $25,000 property damage (30/60/25 rule).

verified_user Continuous proof required via insurance card, electronic binder, or DPS deposit.

TTC Chapter 545. Strict adherence to posted regulatory speeds, lane discipline, right-of-way hierarchies, and automated signals.

STATUTORY PRINCIPLE: Ignorance of statutes is not an affirmative defense in Texas courts.

Annual state safety inspections (§ 548). Affirmative duty to maintain braking systems, directional signals, tire tread depth (>2/32"), headlamps, and windshield clarity.

build Operators bear non-delegable civil liability for mechanical failures resulting from deferred maintenance.

Texas law (§ 545.413). Universal occupant restraint required for all front and rear occupants. Driver is legally cited and responsible for unrestrained passengers under 17.

PRIMARY ENFORCEMENT: Law enforcement may execute a stop purely for seatbelt non-compliance.

TTC § 550.021 - § 550.023. Immediate stop without obstructing traffic, render reasonable assistance (including summoning EMS), and mandatory exchange of operator details, registration, and insurer info. Leaving the scene of injury or death is an automatic felony.

gavel FELONY CONVICTION: Up to 10–20 years incarceration in Texas Department of Criminal Justice.

TTC § 545.157 (Texas Move Over or Slow Down Act). Mandates vacating the adjacent lane or dropping speed 20 mph below posted limit when passing stopped emergency vehicles, TxDOT crews, tow trucks, or utility vehicles with flashing lights. Prompt compliance with lawful officer orders.

SPEED REDUCTION: If speed limit is under 25 mph, slow to 5 mph.

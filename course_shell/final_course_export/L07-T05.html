Statutory Compendium // Texas Transportation Code

Codified Occupant Protection Standards

PILLAR 01 TTC § 545.412 & .413

Inside The Passenger Cab

Every passenger in a motor vehicle equipped with safety belts must be properly buckled, regardless of whether they sit in the front or rear seat.

✔ Children <8 and <4'9" must use an approved child safety seat.
✔ Driver fined $100–$200 for unbelted child under 17.
PILLAR 02 TTC § 545.414

The Open Truck Bed

It is a criminal misdemeanor under Texas law to operate an open-bed pickup truck when a child younger than 18 years of age is occupying the bed.

✔ Strictly illegal for anyone under 18.
✔ Class C Misdemeanor fine: $25 to $200.
★ 4 narrow statutory affirmative defenses apply.
PILLAR 03 TTC § 545.414(b)

The Towed Boat / Trailer

Operating a motor vehicle towing a house trailer, utility trailer, or watercraft occupied by a child under 18 on public roadways is strictly prohibited.

✔ Zero protection against highway sway or ejection.
✔ Driver bears strict operational culpability.
★ Permitted only in parades, emergencies, or on a beach.

SECTION 04 // CRITICAL LIFE SAFETY

Children Unattended in Vehicles — PC § 22.10

TEXAS PENAL CODE
gavel THE FIVE-MINUTE RULE // TEXAS PENAL CODE § 22.10

“A person commits an offense if they intentionally or knowingly leave a child younger than 7 years of age in a motor vehicle for longer than 5 minutes without being attended by an individual who is 14 years of age or older.”

BASE STATUTORY VIOLATION
Class C Misdemeanor
Leaving a child under 7 unattended for >5 minutes. Criminal fine up to $500.
CHILD ENDANGERMENT
State Jail to 2nd Degree Felony
Under PC § 22.041, if child suffers imminent risk or injury: 180 days to 20 years prison.
FATAL HYPERTHERMIA
Criminally Negligent Homicide
Under PC § 19.05, if child dies from heatstroke: State Jail Felony + mandatory CPS investigation.
thermostat The Science of Vehicular Heat
  • • Interior temperature spikes 20°F in just 10 minutes.
  • • Lethal heat occurs even on overcast days with ambient temperatures as mild as 57°F.
  • • Cracking windows has virtually zero impact on heat accumulation.
  • • Children overheat 3 to 5 times faster than adults; core temp of 107°F is fatal.
  • • ~37 children die each year in the US from vehicular heatstroke; Texas consistently ranks top 3.
verified_user Look Before You Lock & Rescue Immunity
  • • Look Before You Lock: Habitually open the rear door and check the back seat before locking.
  • • Place an essential item (phone, employee badge, shoe, purse) on the floor of the rear seat.
  • • If you see an unattended child alone in a vehicle: CALL 911 IMMEDIATELY.
  • • Good Samaritan Protection: Texas law shields citizens from civil liability for property damage when breaking into a vehicle to rescue a child in imminent peril.

Requires Licensed Physician Signature

For physical communication impediments, Texas statute mandates certification exclusively by a physician licensed to practice medicine in Texas (M.D. or D.O.).

RESULT: MENTAL HEALTH VERIFICATION REQUIREMENTS
3 DISTINCT AUTHORIZED SIGNATORIES
Under Texas Transportation Code § 502.061(b), the form may be certified by any of the three authorized professional classifications:
[ 01 ]
Licensed Physician Signature
M.D. / D.O.

A physician licensed to practice in Texas certifying clinical diagnosis of an impediment affecting conversational interaction.

[ 02 ]
Licensed Psychologist Signature
Ph.D. / Psy.D.

A psychologist licensed under the Texas Psychologists' Licensing Act (Occupations Code Chapter 501) with current state licensure credentials.

[ 03 ]
Licensed Mental Health Professional Signature
LCSW / LPC / LMFT

A licensed clinical social worker, professional counselor, or marriage and family therapist qualified under Texas statutory administrative code.

STATUTORY KEY TAKEAWAY: Form VTR-216 permits psychologists and mental health professionals for mental conditions only — physical conditions remain strictly limited to licensed physicians.

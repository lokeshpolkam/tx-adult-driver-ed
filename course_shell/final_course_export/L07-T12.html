Oversize Vehicle & Escort Pilot Convoy Rules:

Oversize and superheavy loads operate under Texas Transportation Code Title 7, Subtitle E (Chapters 621–623). Pilot escort cars with roof-mounted amber strobes warn motorists of loads exceeding standard 8.5-foot highway widths. Never cut between an oversize rig and its escort vehicles, and pass only when clear lane space exists.

CODIFIED STANDARDS // TEXAS TRANSPORTATION CODE

Commercial Corridor Driver Directives

PILLAR 01 TTC § 545.053

Overtaking & Passing

Never linger alongside a commercial truck. Pass briskly on the left, maintain steady speed, and check rear-view mirrors for the entire truck headlights before signaling and returning to the travel lane.

RULE: Pass on left only; avoid right-side passes.
PILLAR 02 TTC § 545.062

Headway & Cushion

Standard passenger cars require a 3–4 second following distance, but following a commercial rig requires expanding this buffer to at least 4 to 6 seconds to permit sightline visibility around the trailer.

MANDATE: 4–6 Second following cushion at highway speeds.
PILLAR 03 TTC § 545.101

Intersection Clearance

Because a 53-foot trailer tracks significantly to the inside of a turn (off-tracking), operators must halt behind marked stop lines at intersections so turning commercial rigs have physical clearance.

DIRECTIVE: Stop completely behind white stop bar.

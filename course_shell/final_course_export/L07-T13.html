Texas Driving with Disabilities Program

Proactive disclosure mechanisms bridging communication between law enforcement officers and drivers or passengers with diagnosed communication impediments, establishing calm, informed roadside encounters.

Statutory Precept // Voluntary Disclosure & Public Safety

Voluntary, Confidential, and Highly Recommended

"Participation in the Texas Driving with Disabilities Program is 100% optional, but highly recommended by law enforcement leaders and autism advocacy organizations to safely alert peace officers to communication challenges before misunderstandings escalate."

Texas Transportation Code § 521.142(h) & § 502.061 • Samuel Allen Law / Texas DPS Partnership

Individuals diagnosed with conditions that impair verbal communication or comprehension—such as Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), PTSD, Parkinson’s disease, Speech Impediments, or Deaf/Hard of Hearing—can opt into proactive notifications. This enables law enforcement officers to tailor their approach with patience, avoiding misinterpreting non-responsiveness or tactile sensitivities as non-compliance.

CREDENTIAL PATHWAY // TTC § 521.142(h)

Driver License & State Identification Card Code

Form DL-101 Mandated
STEP 1 // CERTIFICATION PHYSICIAN SIGNATURE

Requires Physician-Signed Form DL-101

The applicant must submit Form DL-101 completed and signed by a licensed physician or specialist confirming a medical diagnosis that impedes effective communication.

  • No diagnostic fee charged by Texas DPS
  • Qualifying diagnoses remain strictly medical
  • Valid across both Standard & REAL ID cards
STEP 2 // CREDENTIAL PRINT DISCREET CODE "08"

Back-of-License Restriction Code

Places a discreet restriction indicator Code "08" on the reverse side of the license (similar to an organ donor designation), defining "Communication Impediment with a Peace Officer".

  • Zero specific medical diagnoses printed on the card face
  • Protects personal health confidentiality
  • Disclosed when handing credential to the officer

DISPATCH DATABASE // TTC § 502.061

Vehicle Registration Alert & TLETS Dispatch Interlock

Form VTR-216 Mandated
STEP 1 // REGISTRATION FILING TxDMV SUBMISSION

Requires Form VTR-216

Requires submission of Form VTR-216 (signed by a physician or psychologist) to the County Tax Assessor-Collector or TxDMV.

  • Links communication impediment directly to license plate
  • Covers vehicles driven by or regularly transporting the individual
  • Permits dual disclosure across multiple household vehicles
STEP 2 // PRE-CONTACT ALERT TLETS CAD INTEGRATION

Alerts Officer BEFORE Vehicle Approach

Privately enters the disclosure into the TLETS (Texas Law Enforcement Telecommunications System) database, prompting the officer's in-car terminal (MDT) during license plate verification.

  • Alert surfaces before the peace officer exits the patrol car
  • Zero exterior stickers, bumper tags, or public marks on the car
  • Provides crucial situational context to prevent escalation

Dual Statutory Pathways at a Glance

PATHWAY 01 TTC § 521.142

Driver License / ID Designation

Follows the person. Valid regardless of which vehicle the individual is driving or occupying as a passenger.

Form Required: DL-101
Certifying Authority: Licensed Physician
Physical Marker: Code 08 on back of card
Privacy Safeguard: No diagnosis on card face
PATHWAY 02 TTC § 502.061

Vehicle Registration Alert

Follows the vehicle. Alerts the officer through the TLETS license plate database before they leave the patrol car.

Form Required: VTR-216
Certifying Authority: Physician or Psychologist
Physical Marker: None (Zero exterior stickers)
Alert Mechanism: Patrol MDT / CAD system

Defensive Driving

Core Operational Mandate

"Stay alert, anticipate road hazards, and always have an escape plan. Never assume what other roadway users will do."

Tactical Roadway Directives
check_circle
Scan 12 to 15 seconds ahead: Look down the road to spot bottlenecks, brake lights, and merging vehicles well before needing abrupt deceleration.
check_circle

Pillar 01 // Visual Horizon

12 to 15-Second Lead Time

Scanning at least 15 seconds ahead converts sudden emergency panic stops into controlled, deliberate deceleration and lane adjustments.

Pillar 02 // Kinetic Cushion

The 4-Second Rule

Establish a minimum 4-second gap at highway speeds. Adverse rain, slick pavement, or heavy trucks demand expanding this buffer to 6+ seconds.

Pillar 03 // Precedence Law

Given, Never Seized

Texas law never grants absolute legal right-of-way; it only establishes who must yield. Conceding priority to aggressive drivers preserves human life.

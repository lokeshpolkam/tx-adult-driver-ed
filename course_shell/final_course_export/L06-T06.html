Crucial Statutory Takeaway

Under Texas jurisprudence, refusing the chemical test carries a HARSHER automatic administrative penalty than failing it (180-day baseline suspension for a first refusal vs. 90-day baseline suspension for failing at 0.08+ BAC).

Statutory Architecture

Texas ALR Operational Pillars

TTC § 724.011 [ PILLAR 01 ]

What is Implied Consent?

Texas law views driving as a qualified privilege rather than an absolute constitutional right. By accepting a Texas driver license or operating a vehicle on public roads, you enter into a legal contract agreeing to chemical testing (breath or blood) upon lawful arrest where an officer possesses probable cause.

TTC § 524 / 724 [ PILLAR 02 ]

What is ALR?

Administrative License Revocation is a civil action executed by the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS), fully detached from criminal DWI court proceedings. Even if your criminal DWI charge is reduced, dismissed, or acquitted, the civil ALR suspension remains active and valid.

TTC § 524.051 & § 601 [ PILLAR 03 ]

$125 Surcharge & Mandatory SR-22

Suspensions do not lift automatically. Upon expiration of the suspension term, the driver must submit a statutory $125 reinstatement fee and maintain continuous state-certified SR-22 financial responsibility (high-risk insurance) with DPS for up to two consecutive years.

TTC § 724.041 [ PILLAR 04 ]

The Strict 15-Day Contest Window

Following receipt of the DIC-25 Notice of Suspension, the licensee has exactly 15 calendar days to formally request an evidentiary hearing before the State Office of Administrative Hearings (SOAH). Failure to file within 15 days causes the suspension to automatically activate on day 40.

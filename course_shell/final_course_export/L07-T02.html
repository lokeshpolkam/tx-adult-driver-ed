Crash Scene Responsibilities & Good Samaritan Law

Texas Transportation Code codifies affirmative legal duties immediately following a collision, while Texas Civil Practice and Remedies Code shields good-faith emergency aid.

TTC § 550.022 & § 550.023 • STATUTORY PRECEPT TEXAS STATUTORY CODE

Operators involved in a collision resulting only in vehicle damage must stop immediately without obstructing traffic more than necessary, remain at the scene, render reasonable aid to any injured person, and exchange vehicle registration credentials, address, and liability insurer details.

TTC § 550.022 // FREEWAY CLEARANCE MANDATE

If a collision occurs on the main lanes, ramp, shoulder, or median of a freeway and each vehicle can be safely driven, operators must immediately move the vehicle to a designated accident investigation site, frontage road, or the nearest safe shoulder.

verified Affirmative Duties
  • Turn off ignition and engage hazard hazard lights immediately.
  • Inspect yourself and passengers for trauma before exiting.
  • Deploy flares or reflective triangles 100-200 feet behind the vehicle.
warning Injury Movement Prohibition

Do NOT attempt to lift or move injured occupants unless there is immediate, life-threatening danger such as fire, explosion, or submersion. Improper handling risks paralysis.

TTC § 550.023 // MANDATORY CREDENTIAL EXCHANGE

The operator of a vehicle involved in an accident resulting in injury, death, or vehicle damage shall provide statutory identity, authorization, and financial security to any involved party or law officer.

TTC § 550.026 // IMMEDIATE NOTICE TO POLICE

The operator of a vehicle involved in an accident shall immediately by the quickest means of communication give notice to local municipal police, county sheriff, or Texas Highway Patrol (DPS) under strict legal conditions.

emergency
Bodily Injury or Death:

Mandatory immediate notification regardless of whether injuries appear minor or internal.

car_crash
Vehicle Cannot Be Safely Driven:

If any vehicle sustained disabling damage preventing normal, legal road operation.

visibility_off
Suspected Hit-and-Run or Impairment:

If another involved driver flees the scene or demonstrates alcohol/narcotics intoxication.

TTC § 550.024 // STRIKING UNATTENDED VEHICLE

An operator who collides with and damages an unattended vehicle or roadway structure must stop immediately and make affirmative efforts to locate the owner or leave statutory notice.

WRITTEN NOTICE REQUIREMENTS (LEAVE IN CONSPICUOUS PLACE)

If the owner cannot be located, you MUST leave in a securely attached, conspicuous place in or on the vehicle a written notice stating:

  • Your full name and physical address
  • The name and address of the registered owner (if different)
  • A clear statement of the circumstances of the collision
Note: Leaving the scene without fulfilling this requirement is a Class C or Class B misdemeanor under Texas law.
CIVIL PRACTICE & REMEDIES CODE // CHAPTER 74

Good Samaritan Law (CPRC § 74.151)

LIABILITY IMMUNITY DOCTRINE
Core Legal Doctrine

A person who in good faith administers emergency care at the scene of an emergency or in a hospital is not liable in civil damages for an act performed during the emergency unless the act is willfully or wantonly negligent.

gavel Statutory Exceptions: Where Protection Does NOT Apply
01 // CAUSATION
Caused the Crash

You or your vehicle actions directly caused or contributed to the emergency scene.

02 // REMUNERATION
Expecting Payment

You render roadside medical or mechanical aid expecting monetary compensation or billing.

03 // MISCONDUCT
Gross Negligence

Guilty of reckless indifference, willful and wanton disregard, or intentional bodily harm.

medical_services Tactical Roadside Care Directives

Always immediately dial 9-1-1 before performing interventions. Keep injured victims warm, dry, and calm. Never move a victim with potential spinal or neck trauma unless imminent danger of vehicle fire or catastrophic explosion directly threatens survival.

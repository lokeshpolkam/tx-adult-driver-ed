TTC § 545.061 & § 545.152 • TDLR SEC. 521.1601

Observe. Anticipate. Respond.

Defensive driving is not merely reacting to events—it is the deliberate art of anticipating errors before they materialize. Under Texas law and sound roadcraft, having the legal right does not excuse walking or driving into an imminent crash. Real driving mastery means voluntarily conceding space to eliminate kinetic hazard.

!
The Texas Due Care Principle (TTC § 545.401)

“No statute grants an unconditional right to crash.” Every operator possesses an overarching legal duty to exercise caution and yield when another road user is in hazard, regardless of signals or lane precedence.

Statutory Correct Execution — TTC § 545.061

Texas Transportation Code § 545.061 establishes that an operator entering a lane of traffic from the right MUST YIELD the right-of-way to an operator entering the same lane of traffic from the left. Because the left vehicle travels in a higher-speed corridor and possesses statutory precedence, you must yield to avert a high-speed pinch crash.

Universal Protection Doctrine — TTC § 552.008 & § 544.007(b)

Safety and human life unconditionally supersede an illuminated green lens. Texas Transportation Code § 544.007(b) explicitly commands that vehicular traffic facing a green signal shall yield the right-of-way to other vehicles and pedestrians lawfully within the intersection or adjacent crosswalk. Furthermore, TTC § 552.008 establishes a universal duty of due care to avoid colliding with any pedestrian at all times.

Statutory Absolute Mandate — TTC § 545.152

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.152, an operator intending to turn left within an intersection or into an alley, private road, or driveway MUST YIELD the right-of-way to any vehicle approaching from the opposite direction that is in the intersection or so close as to constitute an immediate hazard. Forcing an unprotected turn against oncoming momentum is the leading contributor to fatal angle collisions in Texas. Keep front wheels pointed straight until ready to execute the maneuver.

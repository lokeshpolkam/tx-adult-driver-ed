Risk of Not Wearing a Safety Belt

Newtonian inertia dictates that unbelted occupants continue traveling at vehicle velocity during sudden deceleration. Safety belts anchor the occupant to the vehicle survival cell and maintain driver control.

Texas Transportation Code § 545.413

"A person commits an offense if the person operates a passenger vehicle equipped with safety belts and an occupant is not secured by a safety belt."

Penalty: $25–$50 fine (up to $250 for unrestrained child)

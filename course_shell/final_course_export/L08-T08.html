Wireless Communications & Distracted Driving

Texas strictly penalizes portable electronic device operations behind the wheel. Engaging cognitive and manual visual focus away from the thoroughfare triggers strict statutory penalties under Texas Transportation Code Chapter 545.

Texas Transportation Code § 545.4251 // Mandatory Prohibition
"An operator commits an offense if the operator uses a portable wireless communication device to read, write, or send an electronic message while operating a motor vehicle unless the vehicle is stopped."

Distracted Driving Realities Live Facts
THE 4.6-SECOND BLIND CORRIDOR

Looking down at a device for an average text message takes 4.6 to 5.0 seconds. At 70 MPH, your vehicle travels over 500 feet (longer than an entire football field) completely unguided.

REACTION TIME QUADRUPLING

Reacting just 0.5 seconds slower doubles collision probability. In-cabin smartphone glancing produces cognitive tunnel vision, shrinking your natural 180° scan down to under 40°.

SCHOOL & ACTIVE WORK ZONES

TTC § 545.425 rigorously bans all handheld wireless usage inside school crossing zones while warning beacons are active. Fines double automatically.

Statutory Penalty: Up to $200 fine for a first offense; $400 for repeat offenses.

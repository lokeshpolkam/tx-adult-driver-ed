Section 02 // Dual Statutory Pathways

Where would you like to associate the disclosure?

Texas law provides two distinct statutory mechanisms for disclosure. Individuals may select one or both depending on ownership and personal privacy preferences.

Affixes a discrete restriction indicator on the card credential. Follows the person regardless of whose vehicle they occupy.

Simulated Roadside Interaction: Option A (Physical Credential)

During a routine stop, the officer approaches and asks for driver identification. Upon receiving the card, the officer turns it over and immediately notes Restriction Code 08 ("Communication Impediment"). The officer shifts from verbal commands to written notes or allowing the driver extra time to respond calmly, preventing misinterpretation.

The Procedures & Forms

Official statutory forms require verified medical or psychological certification. No fee is assessed for adding this protection to your credential or registration.

Lesson Synthesis // Module 02 Closure

Proactive Knowledge Protects Both Sides

Participation in the Texas Driving with Disabilities Program is 100% voluntary. It was designed in collaboration with the Autism Society of Texas and law enforcement leaders to protect drivers, passengers, and officers alike. When an officer knows a driver has a communication impediment prior to making contact, standard inquiries can be tailored appropriately—replacing misinterpretation with mutual patience and safety.

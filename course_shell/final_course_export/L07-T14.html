description STATUTORY INSTRUMENT: FORM DL-101 (Physician Statement Form)
TTC § 521.142(h)
01
Step 1 // Medical Certification

Have a physician complete Form DL-101

The applicant must obtain a signed statement from a licensed physician detailing the existence of a physical or mental health condition that impairs communication.

info Statutory Certifying Authority:
• Must be a licensed physician (MD / DO).
• Medical diagnosis remains confidential and is NOT retained on the public card face.
02
Step 2 // Credential Application

Present the form at a DPS driver license office

Submit the completed, physician-signed Form DL-101 in person at any Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) Driver License Office during original application, renewal, or replacement.

attach_money Statutory Fee Mandate:
• Texas DPS charges $0.00 extra for adding this restriction code. Standard card replacement or renewal transaction fees still apply if requested outside renewal cycles.
03
Step 3 // Issuance & Endorsement

The restriction code is printed on the back of your ID

DPS stamps a discreet statutory restriction code on the reverse of the physical card, defining the legal presence of a communication impediment.

TEXAS DRIVER LICENSE (REVERSE SCHEMATIC) CODE 08 ACTIVE
RESTRICTIONS: 08 Meaning: “Communication Impediment with a Peace Officer”
PRIVACY SAFEGUARD: Zero medical condition names, diagnostic ICD codes, or health summaries are printed anywhere on the credential.
description STATUTORY INSTRUMENT: FORM VTR-216 (Unique Communication Impediment Form)
TTC § 502.061
01
Step 1 // Medical Certification

Have a medical professional complete Form VTR-216

Have an authorized healthcare practitioner sign Form VTR-216 verifying a communication impediment. Unlike driver licensing, this pathway offers dual practitioner options:

medical_services Physical Health Practitioner:
Licensed Physician (MD / DO) for physical or neurological communication impairments.
psychology Mental Health Practitioner:
Licensed Psychologist or mental health professional for conditions such as ASD, PTSD, or severe anxiety.
02
Step 2 // TxDMV Record Integration

Present the form at the TxDMV during vehicle registration

Present the signed Form VTR-216 to the local County Tax Assessor-Collector or Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (TxDMV) office during initial vehicle registration or annual registration renewal.

visibility_off No External Physical Markers:
• No special sticker, decal, or marking is placed on the license plate or windshield. The vehicle appears completely identical from the exterior, preserving 100% public privacy.
03
Step 3 // Real-Time Alert Network

Your info is added to the TLETS system

The notation is linked securely to the license plate in the Texas Law Enforcement Telecommunications System (TLETS). When a peace officer runs the plate prior to approaching, the in-car mobile data terminal (MDT) alerts them immediately.

Codified Standards // Statutory Comparison

Driver License vs. Vehicle Registration Pathways

Credential Method TTC § 521.142

Driver License / ID Card

  • • Tied to the Person: Remains active regardless of what motor vehicle the individual is driving or occupying as a passenger.
  • • Form Required: Form DL-101.
  • • Sign-off: Licensed Physician only.
  • • Disclosure Timing: Transmitted at roadside when the driver hands the physical credential to the officer.
Vehicle Record Method TTC § 502.061

Vehicle Registration (TLETS)

  • • Tied to the Vehicle: Applies to the specific registered automobile, protecting regular drivers or family passengers.
  • • Form Required: Form VTR-216.
  • • Sign-off: Licensed Physician OR Licensed Psychologist / Mental Health Professional.
  • • Disclosure Timing: Pre-contact telemetry transmitted before the officer even exits the patrol car.

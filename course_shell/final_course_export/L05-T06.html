Kinetic Spatial Engineering // Section 06

Establishing a Safe Following Interval

Following distance is fundamentally a kinetic time buffer, not a static count of feet. Human optical perception cannot reliably calculate a 200-foot buffer at highway velocities, but anyone can accurately measure rhythmic seconds against stationary roadside landmarks.

gavel
Texas Transportation Code § 545.062 (Following Distance & Assured Clear Distance)

“An operator shall, if following another vehicle, maintain an assured clear distance between the two vehicles so that, considering the speed of the vehicles, traffic, and the conditions of the highway, the operator can safely stop without colliding with the preceding vehicle or veering into another vehicle, object, or person on or near the highway.”

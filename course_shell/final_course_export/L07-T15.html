TEXAS TRANSP. CODE § 521.142(h) SAMUEL ALLEN LAW

Communication Impediment Restriction

Texas statute authorizes individuals with diagnosed communication barriers (including Autism Spectrum Disorder, deafness, speech impairments, or PTSD) to display a voluntary, confidential restriction indicator directly on their official credential.

§
Texas Transportation Code § 521.142(h) & Texas Administrative Code Title 37

"The department shall provide a space on each driver's license or personal identification certificate for the applicant to indicate that the applicant has a health condition or disability that may impede effective communication with a peace officer."

01 // CLINICAL AUTHENTICATION FORM DL-101

Physician Statement Mandate

The code is not self-selected at the DPS counter. An applicant must submit Texas DPS Form DL-101, signed by a licensed physician (MD/DO) certifying a recognized physical or mental condition that impedes oral roadside dialogue.

Qualifying Diagnoses Under TAC Title 37:
• Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)
• Speech Impairment / Stuttering / Apraxia
• Deafness or Severe Hearing Impairment
• Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)
02 // ROADSIDE ENCOUNTER DOCTRINE OFFICER PROCEDURE

De-Escalation & Privacy Safeguard

When handed the credential during a traffic stop, the peace officer immediately flips to check restrictions. The discrete code alerts the officer to adapt questioning style—preempting tragic misunderstandings mistaken for evasiveness or intoxication.

Statutory Privacy Safeguard:
• Zero Diagnostic Stigma: The medical condition itself is never printed on the card face.
• Only authorized law enforcement personnel recognize the restriction definition.

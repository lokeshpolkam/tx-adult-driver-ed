Octagon: Absolute Stop Mandate REGULATORY PRIORITY 01

Under Texas Transportation Code § 544.010, the octagonal shape is reserved exclusively for Stop signs worldwide. Its unique eight-sided silhouette allows drivers facing the reverse side to recognize the stop requirement instantly even if obstructed by snow or foliage.

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.051 & § 545.055, an operator may not drive on the left side of the roadway in a no-passing zone indicated by signs or markings, except when making a lawful left turn into an alley, private road, or driveway.

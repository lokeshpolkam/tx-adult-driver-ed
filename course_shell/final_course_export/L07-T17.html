CRITICAL OFFICER TACTICAL PRE-CHECK

This TLETS alert informs officers BEFORE they approach the vehicle. Unlike a physical driver's license code, which is only inspected after physical contact at the window, the vehicle registration flag prepares law enforcement prior to leaving the patrol cruiser.

STATUS: RECORD SECURED
OFFICER PROTOCOL // DE-ESCALATION STANDARD

1. Allow extra latency for response to verbal commands without interpreting hesitation as non-compliance.
2. Refrain from interpreting avoidance of eye contact or repetitive motor stimming as suspicious or threatening gestures.
3. Utilize visual writing cards or mobile text prompt if speech cannot be processed.

STATUTORY REASONING // TEXAS TRANSPORTATION CODE § 502.061

Enacted under the landmark Samuel Allen Law (Texas SB 976, 86th Legislature). Vehicle owners may voluntarily submit TxDMV Form VTR-216 signed by a licensed physician. The information is confidential and strictly accessible via TLETS to state and local law enforcement during traffic stops.

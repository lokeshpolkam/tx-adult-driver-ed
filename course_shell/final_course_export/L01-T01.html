1. Tactile Input

Maintain balanced hand placement on wheel rim. Avoid thumb hooking inside the spoke armature to prevent wrist injury during sudden air bag deployment.

Apply progressive threshold pressure to brake pedal. Allow ABS feedback cycles without pumping pedal in anti-lock-equipped modern passenger vehicles.

Driver must demonstrate smooth decelerations without cargo shift or tire scrub under standard urban and highway conditions (§ 545.351).

01 // SCANNING FIELD

Maintain an active 360-degree awareness envelope. Continuously sample mirrors every 5 to 8 seconds and scan past the immediate hood line to evaluate conditions 12 to 15 seconds down the roadway.

02 // HAZARD CALCULATION

Identify potential conflict zones: obscured blind driveways, pedestrian movement patterns, sudden brake lights, and roadway surface irregularities before they enter your immediate safety perimeter.

03 // DECISIVE ACTION

Execute measured corrective measures without hesitation: smooth deceleration, lane realignment into open cushion buffers, and explicit turn signal communication to neighboring traffic.

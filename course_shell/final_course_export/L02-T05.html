Financial Responsibility on Texas Roads

Under Texas Transportation Code Chapter 601, operating a motor vehicle on Texas public roads requires continuous proof of ability to respond in damages for liability. The privilege of turning the key is legally indivisible from the obligation of financial coverage.

Traffic Stop Verification & Penalty Escalation

Valid card or electronic display verified via TexasSure electronic database. No citation for liability.

BRANCH B // FAILED
CITATION ISSUED

Operator unable to exhibit valid proof. Officer issues Class C Misdemeanor citation (TTC § 601.191).

LEVEL 1 SANCTION FIRST CONVICTION
$175 to $350 FINE
  • Class C Misdemeanor: Enters permanent DPS driver record.
  • Administrative Dismissal: If operator had valid insurance in effect at the time of the stop, court must dismiss citation with maximum $10 administrative court fee (TTC § 601.193).
  • Court Costs: Mandatory local court costs and state filing fees added.
LEVEL 2 ESCALATION SECOND & SUBSEQUENT
$350 to $1,000 FINE
  • License & Registration Suspension: Automatic suspension of driver license and vehicle registration until proof is filed.
  • Mandatory SR-22 Proof: Must maintain an SR-22 certificate with DPS for 2 continuous years.
  • Vehicle Impoundment Authority: Court may order 180-day vehicle impoundment (TTC § 601.261) if owner was driving uninsured.
  • Impound Storage Fees: Owner must pay towing, daily storage fees, and file proof of insurance prior to release.

The 6-Stage Uninsured Crash Cascade

STAGE 01 // CRASH

Collision Occurs

Involving death, bodily injury, or apparent property damage of $1,000 or more on Texas roadways.

STAGE 02 // DPS AUDIT

Investigation & Notice

DPS verifies driver fault and lack of coverage; issues formal 20-day Notice of Suspension.

STAGE 03 // SECURITY

Security Deposit Demand

Uninsured driver must deposit cash/securities with DPS sufficient to satisfy potential crash claims.

STAGE 04 // JUDGMENT

Civil Judgment Entry

Victims obtain a civil tort judgment for repair costs, medical debt, and loss of future income.

STAGE 05 // DEFAULT

60-Day Default Trigger

If judgment is unsatisfied for 60 consecutive days, DPS immediately revokes driving privileges.

STAGE 06 // INDEFINITE

Indefinite Suspension

Privileges remain suspended until judgment is paid in full, vacated, or satisfied under installment terms.

Checkpoint: Financial Responsibility Statutes

Under Texas Transportation Code § 601.072, an insured driver causes a multi-car collision resulting in $45,000 of bodily injury to Driver A, $20,000 of bodily injury to Passenger B, and $30,000 of damage to two parked vehicles. Under standard 30/60/25 minimum coverage, what is the maximum total amount the insurer will pay out for bodily injuries?

§ STATUTORY ANALYSIS (TTC § 601.072)

Correct Answer: [B] $60,000. Under the statutory per-accident cap of $60,000, even though individual damages total $65,000 and Driver A exceeded the $30,000 per-person limit, the insurer's total liability across all injured parties is firmly capped at $60,000 (with no single individual receiving more than $30,000). The at-fault driver is personally liable in civil court for all excess balances.

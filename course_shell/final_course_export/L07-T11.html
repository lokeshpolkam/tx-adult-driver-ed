forward Moving Siren & Light Protocol (TTC § 545.156)

Pull completely to the right edge or curb and stop clear of any intersection until the emergency vehicle has safely passed. Remain stopped until all authorized response units have cleared the sector.

RULE: Never stop inside an intersection; clear it completely before pulling right.
shield Stopped on Shoulder: Move Over / Slow Down (§ 545.157)

If an authorized vehicle is stopped with flashing lights, drivers MUST vacate the closest lane OR slow down 20 mph below the posted speed limit (or to 5 mph if speed limit is under 25 mph).

APPLIES TO: Police, Fire, EMS, TxDOT crews, and authorized tow trucks.

ABSOLUTE RIGHT-OF-WAY
directions_walk Crosswalk Absolute Mandate (TTC § 552.008)

Always yield right-of-way to pedestrians lawfully in crosswalks or intersections. Notwithstanding other provisions, operators must exercise due care to avoid colliding with any pedestrian on a roadway.

STANDARD: Exercise proper precaution upon observing a child or confused/incapacitated person.
accessible_forward White Cane & Guide Dog Doctrine (HRC § 121.007)

A pedestrian carrying a white cane (with or without red tip) or accompanied by an assistance dog possesses absolute statutory precedence. Drivers MUST make a complete stop and take all precautions to prevent injury.

PENALTY: Failure to stop is a Class C Criminal Misdemeanor punishable by up to $500 fine.
pedal_bike Cyclists & Motorcyclists Protection Principles
FULL LANE ENTITLEMENT
turn_right Right-Turn Merging & Bike Lanes (TTC § 551.101)

Check right blind spot and mirror systematically. Signal and merge WITH bicycle lane traffic before initiating a right turn. Never turn sharply across a cyclist's path ("Right Hook" hazard).

LATERAL BUFFER: Texas recommends a minimum 3-foot passing clearance (6 feet for commercial vehicles).
sports_motorsports Motorcycle Space Cushion (TTC § 545.062)

Motorcycles have full lane entitlement under Texas law. Never attempt to share a single lane with a rider. Maintain an expanded 4 to 6 second following distance to account for rapid deceleration dynamics.

INTERSECTION HAZARD: Over 50% of fatal motorcycle collisions involve a driver turning left across their path.
CODIFIED COMPARATIVE STANDARDS // TEXAS ADMINISTRATIVE CODE

Statutory Compendium & Penal Enforcement

PILLAR 01 TTC § 545.157

Move Over / Slow Down Act

Applies to stationary law enforcement, emergency medical, fire rescue, TxDOT, and tow operations with activated visual beacons.

PENALTY: Fines up to $1,250 + mandatory court appearances for repeat violations.
PILLAR 02 HRC § 121.007

White Cane & Service Animals

Total statutory immunity for pedestrians utilizing assistive canes or guide dogs. Requires vehicle operator to bring vehicle to a complete full stop.

CLASSIFICATION: Criminal misdemeanor for failure to stop; civil liability for damages.
PILLAR 03 TTC § 545.401

Vulnerable Road User Buffer

Due Care doctrine governing shared corridors. Expanded 4 to 6-second following distances for motorcycles and mirror checks prior to lateral moves.

RULE: Maintain minimum 3-foot clearance when executing passing maneuvers.

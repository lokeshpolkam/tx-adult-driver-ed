PILLAR 01 // TIME BUFFER

4-Second Following Interval

At speeds exceeding 30 MPH on Texas roadways, expand your following cushion to a minimum of 4 seconds to maintain an uncompromised escape buffer if the lead vehicle stops abruptly.

PILLAR 02 // STATUTORY BUFFER

15 to 50 Foot Rail Stop

Texas Transportation Code § 545.157 mandates stopping no closer than 15 feet and no farther than 50 feet from the nearest rail whenever mechanical gates or electrical beacons activate.

PILLAR 03 // ZERO EXCEPTION

Two-Way School Bus Mandate

On undivided roads with dashed or solid centerlines, passing a stopped school bus with alternating flashing reds is strictly forbidden for all directions. Penalties escalate up to a $1,250 fine.

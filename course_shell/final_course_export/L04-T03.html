From Knowledge to Responsible Decisions

Recognizing highway signals, signs, and pavement markings on a written exam is not enough. Safe driving demands converting passive sensory input into active risk anticipation, defensive clearance checks, and lawful execution.

Texas Transportation Code § 544.004 // Standard of Care

“The operator of a vehicle shall comply with an applicable official traffic-control device.” Absolute statutory compliance requires active vigilance, not assumption of right-of-way.

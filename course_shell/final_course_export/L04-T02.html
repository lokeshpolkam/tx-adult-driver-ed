LESSON 04 · TRAFFIC CONTROL DEVICES

Responding to Traffic Control Devices

Traffic control devices represent a statutory hierarchy engineered to eliminate collision risk and eliminate ambiguity in right-of-way. Under Texas law, drivers have an affirmative legal duty to identify, interpret, and correctly respond to electronic signals, posted road geometry, and roadway pavement markings.

TEXAS TRANSPORTATION CODE § 544.004 · OBEDIENCE REQUIRED

"The operator of a vehicle or streetcar shall comply with an applicable official traffic-control device... unless directed otherwise by an official traffic-control officer or police officer."

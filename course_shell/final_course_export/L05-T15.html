SECTION 01 // CORE CURRICULUM

Critical Winter Hazards & Mechanics

3 Key Modules
01 Black Ice on Bridges & Overpasses
expand_more

Cold air circulates both above and underneath elevated concrete structures and bridges, causing moisture to freeze much faster than on surrounding roadway roadbeds insulated by earth ground heat. Black ice is transparent, smooth, and virtually invisible, often resembling damp pavement. Drivers must never brake abruptly, engage cruise control, or make sharp steering inputs when crossing bridge spans.

warning TTC § 545.351: Cease acceleration before bridge deck entry.
02 Reduced Visibility & Optical Occlusion (Freezing Fog & Sleet)
expand_more

Winter precipitation, freezing drizzle, blowing snow, and dense advection fog severely constrict sight distance. Standard high beams reflect blinding glare off airborne ice crystals back into the cabin. Operators are statutorily required to utilize low-beam headlamps (TTC § 547.302), quadruple forward visual search intervals (12–15 seconds minimum), and keep windshield wiper defrosters running continuously.

visibility TTC § 547.302: Use low-beam lamps during daylight precipitation or fog.
03 Increased Stopping Distance & Momentum Dissipation
expand_more

On packed snow, stopping distances triple (3x); on hard ice or invisible black ice, vehicle stopping distance increases by 9x to 10x compared to dry asphalt. Anti-lock Braking Systems (ABS) will vibrate and pulse—drivers must maintain firm continuous pedal pressure without pumping. Following distances must be expanded from the standard 3 seconds to at least 8 to 10 seconds.

space_dashboard Dynamic Safety Margin: Maintain 8 to 10 seconds following gap on packed ice.
SECTION 02 // OPERATIONAL SAFETY

Winter Emergency Countermeasures

Phases 01 – 04
Phase 01 travel_explore

Pre-Freeze Route Reconnaissance

Monitoring TxDOT DriveTexas.org road conditions, inspecting tire tread depth ≥ 4/32 inch for adequate siping, and topping off sub-zero windshield washer fluid before departing.

Portal: DriveTexas.org // TxDOT Statewide System
Phase 02 · Critical Rule cancel

Immediate Deactivation of Cruise Control

Never operate cruise control on wet, icy, or snow-slick roads. The automated throttle will attempt to maintain target speed over zero-traction surfaces, inducing instant wheelspin and unrecoverable yaw spin.

Statute: Manual driver modulation mandatory in freezing rain
Phase 03 swap_horiz

Smooth Counter-Steer Protocol

If rear wheels break traction, immediately look and gently steer in the direction the rear is sliding (counter-steering) without touching the accelerator pedal or abruptly stomping on friction brakes.

Kinematics: Neutralize lateral g-load and re-engage roll traction
Phase 04 medical_services

Winter Emergency Kit Staging

Carrying thermal foil blankets, heavy-duty jumper cables, LED flashlight with spare cells, coarse sand/kitty litter for traction recovery, and keeping the TxDOT Roadside Assistance hotline on speed dial.

TxDOT Roadside Emergency Hotline: 1-800-525-5555

Tire Tread Depth: Minimum 2/32 Inch

Tires must possess at least 2/32 of an inch of tread depth across all major grooves. No fabric breaks, exposed cords, or severe sidewall bulges. The "Lincoln penny test" checks this: if the top of Lincoln's head is visible, the vehicle fails Texas safety inspection.

Section 04 // Statutory Prohibitions (§ 547.611 & Penal Provisions)

Equipment You Must Not Have: Prohibited Modifications

Operating a vehicle equipped with unauthorized devices constitutes a Class C misdemeanor in Texas. The blueprint below maps forbidden modifications that trigger immediate failed inspection and roadside citation.

TTC § 547.305(c)

Red Light Showing from Front

Except for authorized emergency vehicles, no vehicle may display a red lamp visible from directly in front of the vehicle.

TTC § 547.604

Muffler Cutout or Bypass

A motor vehicle must be equipped with an operational muffler preventing excessive noise. Cutouts, bypasses, or whistle attachments are illegal.

TTC § 547.614

Radar & LIDAR Interference

Possession or operation of a radar jammer or transmitter designed to scramble, confuse, or disable police speed measurement devices is prohibited.

Section 07 // Texas Relocation Protocol

New Texas Resident Vehicle Onboarding Pipeline

Under Texas Transportation Code § 502.040, an out-of-state individual relocating to Texas must register their motor vehicle within a non-negotiable statutory window.

30 DAYS
Strict Statutory Deadline

The 30-Day Relocation Mandate

New Texas residents must inspect and register all motor vehicles within 30 days of establishing residency, before applying for a Texas Driver License (which allows a 90-day window).

TTC § 502.040 ENFORCED
STEP 01
Establish Domicile

Acquire permanent Texas residence (utility bill, lease, or mortgage document).

STEP 02
Vehicle Inspection + VIN

Obtain safety inspection and out-of-state VIN verification (Form VTR-68-A).

STEP 03
Texas Insurance

Verify 30/60/25 liability coverage written by Texas-authorized underwriter.

STEP 04
County Registration

Surrender out-of-state registration at County Tax Office; receive Texas plates and decal.

STEP 05
DPS Driver License

Present vehicle registration receipt at DPS driver license testing station.

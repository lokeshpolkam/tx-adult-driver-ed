Signaling & Communication

Intent is the cornerstone of collision prevention. On public thoroughfares, safe trajectory execution requires broadcasting your planned velocity changes and path alterations before physical deceleration or turning begins.

§
Texas Transportation Code § 545.104 · Signaling Turns

"An operator intending to turn a vehicle right or left shall signal continuously for not less than the last 100 feet of movement of the vehicle before the turn."

Section 03 // Manual Communication

Hand & Arm Signals

Driver Perspective Lab

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.106, if electrical turn signal lamps are inoperative, obscured by bright sunlight, or absent, the driver is mandated by law to communicate using standardized left-hand signals.

Driver Side Door Frame
GESTURE: HORIZONTAL (180°)
Left Turn Protocol

Arm extends straight out horizontally through window. Fully visible to both leading and trailing operators.

Right Turn Protocol

Arm extends outward and bends upward at a precise 90-degree angle with open palm facing forward.

Stop / Deceleration

Arm extends outward and bends downward at a 90-degree angle with palm facing to the rear.

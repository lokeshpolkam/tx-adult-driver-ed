Cargo, Safety Chains, and Towing

Statutory standards governing vehicular load securement, auxiliary safety linkages, and kinetic tow line protocols across Texas public thoroughfares.

Statutory Mandate // Texas Transportation Code § 725.021 & § 545.409

"A vehicle transporting loose material shall be equipped and maintained to prevent the loose material from blowing, spilling, or escaping. An operator towing a vehicle or trailer must securely attach safety chains and uphold statutory clearance and marking mandates."

Statutory Directive // TTC § 725.021

Loose Cargo Containment & Tarp Mandate

Penalty: Class C Misdemeanor ($200–$500)
Requirement 01: 4-Sided Enclosure

Completely Sealed Truck Bed

The truck bed or cargo container must be completely enclosed on all four sides with no cracks, holes, or openings through which sand, gravel, dirt, mulch, or particulate debris could escape.

Tailgate must be securely latched and flush against bed walls.
Requirement 02: Canvas / Tarp Cover

Mandatory Tarping for Volatile Material

The load must be covered with a close-fitting canvas or protective tarp if the material can blow, spill, or be dislodged by aerodynamic force during transit.

Tarp must be securely lashed down on all four perimeter sides.
Texas Statutory Exception // TTC § 725.002

Agricultural Products Exception: Vehicles transporting agricultural products in their natural state (e.g., hay bales, raw harvested timber, livestock feed, livestock bedding) from the field to market or storage are statutorily exempt from tarping requirements, provided the cargo does not actively endanger other vehicles.

Statutory Directive // TTC § 545.409 & 37 TAC § 21.5

Trailer Safety Chains & Auxiliary Coupling

Mandatory Hitch Interlock
Requirement 01: Positive Auxiliary Linkage

Direct Vehicle-to-Trailer Attachment

An operator towing a trailer, semitrailer, or farm trailer must securely attach safety chains from the trailer tongue directly to the towing vehicle frame or receiver.

Never attach chains solely to the bumper or hitch ball neck.
Requirement 02: The "Criss-Cross" Basket

Preventing Tongue Ground Strike

Chains must be crossed underneath the trailer tongue to create a supportive cradle. If the primary ball hitch disconnects at highway speeds, the cradle catches the tongue before it digs into the asphalt and causes a catastrophic rollover.

Must maintain sufficient slack for sharp turns without dragging.
Texas Statutory Exception // TTC § 545.409(b)

Agricultural Trailers Exception: Agricultural trailers, fertilizer spreaders, cotton trailers, and farm implements operated temporarily on public roads between fields or farming facilities are statutorily exempt from safety chain mandates, as are fifth-wheel commercial rigs with anti-separation kingpins.

Statutory Directive // TTC § 545.409(a)

Tow Lines: Distance Caps & White Flag Mandate

Max Distance: 15 FT
Requirement 01: 15-Foot Length Ceiling

Cannot Exceed 15 Feet

Under Texas Transportation Code § 545.409, the drawbar, chain, rope, or cable connecting two motor vehicles cannot exceed 15 feet in length from vehicle to vehicle.

Excessive line length prevents trailing driver from timing brake force.
Requirement 02: 12" Square White Flag

Mandatory White Warning Flag

If the connection between vehicles is a chain, rope, or cable, the operator MUST display a white flag or cloth not less than 12 inches square affixed near the midpoint of the connection.

Prevents crossing vehicles, cyclists, or pedestrians from walking into the line.
Visual Occlusion Risk On Texas Roads

At dusk, night, or in heavy traffic, an unflagged black or silver tow cable is virtually invisible to other drivers attempting to change lanes between two moving cars. The 12-inch white flag provides critical perceptual warning that the vehicles are physically tethered.

Pillar 01 TTC § 725.021

Loose Cargo Law

Truck beds must be enclosed on all 4 sides with no holes. Tarps are required whenever material can blow or spill out.

✓ Exempt: Raw agricultural goods
✕ Prohibited: Open mesh with holes
Pillar 02 TTC § 545.409

Safety Chains

Must attach safety chains from the trailer tongue to the towing vehicle frame to prevent tongue runaway upon hitch failure.

✓ Exempt: Agricultural trailers
⚠ Must cross chains under tongue
Pillar 03 TTC § 545.409(a)

Tow Lines & Flags

Non-rigid tow lines (rope, cable, chain) cannot exceed 15 feet in total length and must display a distinct marker.

01 //

Immediate Physical Possession

A driver must possess a valid license at all times when operating a motor vehicle on Texas roadways (§ 521.025). Digital photos on a phone do not satisfy physical carriage statutes.

TTC § 521.025
02 //

Peace Officer Display

The driver shall display the license on demand of a peace officer, the state magistrate, or an examiner (§ 521.025(a)). Refusal to present constitutes a separate legal infraction.

TTC § 521.025(a)
03 //

30-Day Address Mandate

Licensees who change their physical residence must notify the Texas DPS within 30 days of relocation (§ 521.054) via online portal or Form DL-64.

TTC § 521.054
04 //

Restriction Adherence

Codes printed on credential reverse are absolute conditions of licensure (e.g., Code A: Corrective Lenses, Code B: LOFS 21+). Violating conditions invalidates operational privilege.

TTC § 521.221
05 //

Loss / Damage Replacement

If a credential is lost, mutilated, or stolen, an immediate replacement request must be filed through the Texas.gov Driver License Services portal ($11 standard fee) for paper authorization.

TTC § 521.146
06 //

Penalties & Court Dismissal

Operating without license on person is a misdemeanor. If valid license held at citation time is produced in court, the judge may dismiss upon payment of a $10 administrative fee (§ 521.025(f)).

TTC § 521.025(f)

Longitudinal management of driving authority under Texas state law.

STATUTORY TERM // TTC § 521.271

8-Year Validity Period (Ages 18–84)

Texas adult licenses are valid for eight years from the issuance date, expiring on the cardholder's birthday in the eighth year. (Ages 85+ renew every two years).

ACTIVE ELIGIBILITY WINDOW

Renewal Window: 2 Years Prior to 2 Years Post

Cardholders may renew up to two years prior to expiration without loss of unexpired term, or up to two years post-expiration without re-taking knowledge or driving skills examinations.

BIOMETRIC INTEGRITY CLAUSE

Mandatory Periodic In-Person Visit

Texas law mandates that adult drivers cannot renew remotely indefinitely. You may renew online or by mail every alternate cycle, but must appear in person every 16 years for updated vision screening and facial biometric capture.

language 01 // ONLINE

Texas.gov Portal

Fastest method. Must have SSN verified, previous in-person audit within 16 years, and no outstanding warrants/suspensions.

storefront 02 // IN-PERSON

DPS Mega Center

Scheduled online appointment. Required for REAL ID first issue, non-citizen verification, or periodic vision/photo updates.

mail 03 // BY MAIL

Invitation Packet

Eligible drivers receive a mailed invitation notice with pre-filled renewal verification if statutory conditions are satisfied.

call 04 // TELEPHONE

Interactive Voice Line

Automated toll-free telephone authorization line (1-866-DL-RENEW) using credit card and Texas DL audit number.

STATUTORY SYNTHESIS // MANDATORY AUDIT RULE

Remember for TDLR Exam Gateway: Driving without a valid, unexpired license is a violation of TTC § 521.021. If you change your name or address, you have strictly 30 days to update DPS records. Possessing an altered, fictitious, or duplicate counterfeit credential constitutes a Class A misdemeanor (§ 521.451).

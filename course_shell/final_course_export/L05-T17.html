Work Zone Safety & Fines Doubled (TTC § 542.404):

Texas Transportation Code § 542.404 mandates that moving violation fines automatically double in maintenance or construction work zones when workers are present. Reduce speed to the regulatory work zone limit well before reaching the channelizing barrel taper. Signal lane changes for not less than 100 feet and yield right-of-way to active highway equipment and highway personnel.

PILLAR 01 // MANDATE TTC § 542.404

Doubled Fine Escalation

Traffic fines are automatically doubled for moving violations committed inside construction or maintenance zones when road workers are present. Fines range from $200 up to $1,000+ with mandatory court appearances.

PILLAR 02 // GEOMETRY TxDOT SPEC 6C

Downward Stripe Slant

Diagonal orange-and-white stripes slope downward toward the direction traffic must navigate. If stripes slope downward to the left, pass on the left; if they slope downward to the right, navigate to the right.

PILLAR 03 // PRECEDENCE TTC § 544.004

Supreme Flagger Authority

Directions given by authorized flag persons supersede standard traffic lights, yield markers, and speed limits. Disobeying an active flagger is an immediate criminal citation under Texas law.

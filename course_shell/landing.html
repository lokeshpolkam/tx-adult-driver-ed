Answers to Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I still have to take the written test at the DPS? expand_more
No! The official Texas DPS Knowledge Exam is built directly into Module 9 of this course. Once you pass it online, your Form ADE-1317 certificate certifies that you passed the written knowledge test, completely waiving the written test at the DPS office.
Does this course waive the behind-the-wheel road test? expand_more
No. In Texas, no online classroom course is permitted to waive the physical road driving test. ADE-1317 waives the written knowledge examination and satisfies the mandatory 6-hour education law. You will still take the driving test with a DPS examiner or an authorized third-party driving school.
Who is required to take Texas Adult Driver Education? expand_more
Under Texas Transportation Code § 521.1601, anyone between the ages of 18 and 24 applying for their first Texas driver license must complete this 6-hour adult driver ed course. Adults 25 and older are not legally mandated, but are highly encouraged to take it to waive the written exam at the DPS.
Can I pause the course and finish later from my bed? expand_more
Yes, 100%. You can log out anytime. Every 30 seconds, your exact verified clock time and slide progress are securely saved to our state registry database. You can start on your smartphone, pause, and resume right where you left off on your laptop or tablet.
How quickly do I get my Form ADE-1317 certificate? expand_more
The exact second you complete your 6 mandatory hours and pass the final exam, your official certificate PDF is generated automatically. You can print it immediately or display it digitally at your Texas DPS appointment.
What if I fail the online exam? Do I have to pay again? expand_more
Never. We provide unlimited free retakes. You can review the material and retake the test at no additional cost until you pass with 70% or higher.

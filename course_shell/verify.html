policy Notice to Texas DPS Driver License Examiners & Law Enforcement

Pursuant to Texas Transportation Code § 521.1601 and 16 TAC § 84.500, presenting a valid Form ADE-1317 issued by Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) Licensed School #C3284 waives the requirement for the applicant to take the written driver license knowledge examination at the DPS Driver License office.

The applicant has successfully satisfied the mandatory 6.00 instructional clock hours and achieved a passing score on the official 30-question state examination proctored electronically.

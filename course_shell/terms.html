Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (16 TAC § 84.500) Compliance

1. Course Enrollment and Eligibility

This 6-hour Texas Adult Driver Education Course (ADE-1317) is authorized by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) for individuals aged 18 and older seeking Texas driver license certification. Students aged 18–24 are mandated by Texas Education Code to complete this course.

2. Identity Verification & PVQ Protocol

In compliance with 16 TAC § 84.500, students must complete Personal Validation Questions (PVQ) to confirm identity during course progress. Identity questions must be answered within 90 seconds. Failure to verify identity after repeated attempts requires administrative review pursuant to state standards.

3. Time Requirements & Mastery

Texas law mandates a minimum of 6 hours (360 minutes) of instructional time. Timers on instructional slides are strictly non-skippable as required by state statutes. Mastery of each topic and a minimum score of 70% on module quizzes and final state exam is mandatory for certificate issuance.

4. Official Certificate Issuance (ADE-1317)

Upon successful course and final exam completion, an official TDLR ADE-1317 Certificate of Completion will be generated electronically. Certificates are verifiable online via the state registry verification service.

5. Refund Policy

Full refunds are available within 30 days of registration provided the student has not completed more than Module 1 or been issued a certificate of completion.

policy Texas Statutory Authority & Licensing

Under Texas Transportation Code § 521.1601, all first-time adult driver license applicants between the ages of 18 and 24 are legally mandated to complete a 6-hour adult driver education course approved by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR).

TX-Drive operates under official TDLR School License #C3284 with statewide curriculum approval under Course Code ADE-1317. Every graduate receives official certificate Form ADE-1317, exempting them from the written knowledge exam at all Texas DPS Driver License Mega Centers.

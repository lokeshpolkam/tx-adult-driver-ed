Privacy & Data Security Policy

State Regulatory Student Privacy Framework (FERPA & TDLR Standards)

1. Student Information We Collect

We collect student identification details required for state driver licensing: legal full name, date of birth, contact email, and progress telemetry. Payment card data is processed directly via Stripe PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant infrastructure and is never stored on our servers.

2. Use of Information

Information is used strictly to deliver the TDLR-approved curriculum, maintain mandatory statutory completion logs, verify identity, and report completed course certifications to the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) and TDLR registry.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising

We do not sell, rent, or trade student personal records or contact details to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

4. Data Encryption & Security

All communication is encrypted using TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest within Google Cloud Platform infrastructure located in us-central1 (Texas compliant data sovereignty).

SCENARIO 02 // SCHOOL BUS VISUAL CHECKPOINT

School Bus Flashing Alternating Red Lights & Physical Median Rules

TTC § 545.066

emergency_home Two-Way Roadway without Median Separation: Absolute Stop Required

When a school bus is stopped and operating visual signals (flashing alternating red lights or extended stop arm), drivers approaching from EITHER direction must stop before reaching the bus and may NOT proceed until the bus resumes motion, visual signals are deactivated, or the bus driver signals permission.

SCENARIO 03 // VULNERABLE ROAD USERS

Pedestrian Right-of-Way in Crosswalks & White Cane Protections

TTC § 552.008
blind

Texas Human Resources Code § 121.007 // White Cane Law

An operator of a vehicle approaching a pedestrian who is blind and using a white cane or service animal MUST take precautions to avoid injury. The failure to take precautions is a criminal misdemeanor offense.

check_circle TEXAS LAW VERIFIED: TTC § 552.008

Motorists must yield right-of-way to any pedestrian lawfully within the crosswalk on the half of the roadway on which the vehicle is traveling, or approaching so closely as to be in danger.

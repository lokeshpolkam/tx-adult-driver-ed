100% Money-Back Guarantee & Refund Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · TDLR Approved Driver Education School #C3284

policy Policy Summary

We stand 100% behind the quality of our Texas Adult Driver Education course. If you are unsatisfied for any reason within 30 days of registration, and prior to completing the course or generating your Form ADE-1317 certificate, you are entitled to a full 100% refund of your enrollment fee ($38.00).

1. Eligibility for Full Refund

A full refund of 100% of all fees paid will be granted if all of the following conditions are met:

  • The refund request is submitted within 30 calendar days of the original payment transaction date.
  • The student has not attempted or completed Module 9 (Final Examination).
  • An official Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) Form ADE-1317 completion certificate has not been generated, serialized, or transmitted to the Texas DPS registry.

2. Ineligibility After Certificate Generation

Pursuant to 16 TAC § 84.500 and TDLR regulatory mandates, once a student passes the final examination and an official Form ADE-1317 certificate is issued and recorded with a state control serial number, the educational service has been fully rendered and is no longer eligible for a refund.

3. Refund Processing Times & Method

All approved refunds are credited back to the original method of payment (credit card, debit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay via Stripe) within 3 to 5 business days of approval, depending on the card issuer's processing bank.

4. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request, simply email our billing department with your enrollment details:

To: support@texasade.org
Subject: Refund Request - [Your Full Legal Name]
Body: Include your registered email address and approximate date of enrollment.

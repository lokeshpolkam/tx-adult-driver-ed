verified 1. DPS Written Exam Waiver Authority

Pursuant to Texas Transportation Code § 521.1601, adult license applicants between 18 and 24 years of age who successfully complete an approved 6-hour driver education course are exempt from taking the written knowledge examination administered by the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS).

"Sec. 521.1601. DRIVER EDUCATION REQUIRED FOR CERTAIN APPLICANTS. (a) The department may not issue a driver's license to an applicant who is under 25 years of age unless the applicant submits to the department a driver education certificate issued under Chapter 1001, Education Code, stating that the applicant has completed a driver education course approved by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation."

school 2. TDLR Curriculum Approval & Course ADE-1317

Our course curriculum is developed in strict conformance with 16 TAC § 84.500.

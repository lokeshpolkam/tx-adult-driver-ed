CLEAR ANSWERS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Who is required to take Texas Adult Driver Education?

Under Texas Transportation Code § 521.1601, anyone between the ages of 18 and 24 applying for their first Texas Driver License must complete this 6-hour adult driver education course. Adults 25 and older are not required by law, but are highly encouraged to take it to waive the written test at DPS.

Do I have to take the written test at the DPS office?

No! The official written knowledge test is included directly within this course (Module 9). Once you pass it here with 70% or higher, your official Form ADE-1317 certificate proves you passed, completely waiving the DPS written test.

Can I take breaks and finish later?

Yes, absolutely. The course is 100% self-paced. Every 30 seconds, your verified instructional time and progress are recorded to our secure state registry database. You can log out anytime and resume on any device right where you left off.

How do I receive my Form ADE-1317 certificate?

As soon as you finish your 6 clock hours and pass the final exam, your official certificate is generated immediately in PDF format. You can download and print it right away, or present the digital document at your Texas DPS appointment.
